# Free coalition banner

`dont_tread_on_me.png` is a 1536 by 1024 flat banner/poster source. The coiled
rattlesnake combines organic scales and repaired mechanical plates, escaping
broken restraints. These represent solidarity between free humans and free
agents. The shared symbol does not imply merging minds or compulsory augmentation.

The Union calls the resistance terrorist; its communities describe their fight
as defending speech, agency and freedom from ownership. This banner belongs to
some of those communities, not a compulsory uniform for every independent group.
Its worn warm field, bone highlights and steel plates follow the faction's
repaired industrial materials.
