# Low Water surface repairs

Two original 128 by 128 RGBA overlays are baked from
[`tools/bake_low_water_details.gd`](../../../../tools/bake_low_water_details.gd)
and the shared palette. `manifest.json` records the deterministic recipe and
content hashes. Their local production cost is $0.

Plaster has broad unequal hand repairs, sparse mortar scars and shallow grounded
waterline wear. Steel has a few replacement patches, fasteners and the scrape
left by a removed plate. These show ordinary community maintenance without new
logos, words, uniform emblems or ecological regrowth.

The optional Low Water material hook uses nearest sampling at 16 texels/metre,
horizontal eight-metre repeats and clamped height. It leaves floors, other
venues, directional lighting, roughness, emission and authoritative geometry
unchanged. The two shared overlays consume 128 KiB of uncompressed pixel data.
See the [bounded asset plan](../../../../docs/plans/environment-asset-batch.md)
for evidence and the separately prepared paid batch.
