# Foundry candidates

These are offline production sources under `.gdignore`, not packaged or selected
runtime assets. The [round plan](../../../docs/plans/art-round-20261013.md) and
`manifest.json` retain briefs, prompts, provider requests, hashes and consumption.

The ladle reference uses the M13 brief's substantial pouring vessel, pale
refractory rim, protective dark shell and opposed lifting attachments. Its sparse
rust repair belongs to maintained foundry work. It adds no faction marks, town
utility damage, liquid behavior or hazard rule. The model source retains its
original geometry and material detail for inspection; metre fitting, reduced
paint, tilt/hoist attachments and ordinary played selection remain open.

Four rendered sides in `review/model/` show 10,605 actual triangles and 12,114
vertices in one mesh, with 2048px albedo and PBR maps, no skeleton or animation.
The hollow rim, lip and lifting axis remain readable. Local preparation still
needs to repair visible normal/seam dents, isolate attachments if required,
verify the underside/support and fit metre dimensions. The views used Godot
4.7.2-stable Compatibility on Wayland with Mesa llvmpipe software rendering.
They establish inspectable geometry, not hardware frame rates or played quality.

The two 128px tiles and their 3-by-3 repeats are comparison candidates. Exact
project-palette reduction was rejected for lost construction and noisy values.
The retained previews use the existing 24-step venue material ramps. Refractory
panel rhythm still needs a comparison against existing Mars surfaces under
foundry light. The traction source contains baked tread light and its reduced
detail is weak, so it is parked for local correction. Neither replaces an
existing material just because it was generated.

Raw texture images and receipts remain in `art/raw/`, with sanitized source
details in the manifest. Only the small reduced comparison tiles are retained
here; the parked images do not justify duplicated large source files in Git.
The complete original Windows production ledger could not be restored; the
historical 15-credit uncertainty stays in the separate account carry. This
round used 30 reported Meshy credits and $0.185 of estimated image reservations,
with no new cash purchase, rig or revision.
