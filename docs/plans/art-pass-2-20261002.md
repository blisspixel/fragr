## Method

Unchanged from the first pass, which worked: edits of approved keepers with
the keeper as reference, a flat magenta `#FF00FF` field removed by
`reduce --key ff00ff`, palette lock to `docs/palette.json` in L\*a\*b\*, hard
alpha, PNG with image chunks only. New for this pass:

- **Flat colour fields.** Prompts for anything that reduces to small sizes ask
  for broad flat areas in the exact palette families (institutional
  grey-green, gunmetal, black steel) and no grain, speckle or weathering
  noise, because noise between two swatches is what made the Rifle blotchy.
- **Letters and lamp states are local.** Generators draw letters badly, and a
  lamp state must register exactly with its body. Site letters A and B and the
  charge and mine lamp states are composited in a GDScript bake over the
  generated body, so every state frame registers on the same canvas.
- **Effects are baked in GDScript.** Muzzle flashes and impacts are small,
  fast and must be palette exact; a deterministic bake (`tools/bake_shot_vfx.gd`)
  draws them in palette colours with a shape per weapon and frames per impact.
- **Sub-palettes.** A picture is quantised to the subset of `docs/palette.json`
  its subject uses (`tools/spritegen/specs/palettes/`): free-side gear to
  bone, leather, ember and steel; Union issue to black steel, red and bone.
  Cream and tan shading otherwise lands on greens and cyans no part of the
  object has. A majority-vote downscale was tried and dropped: it kept
  outlines crisper but scattered outline pixels through flat areas.
- **Chunky subjects.** A pickup-sized subject is asked for as "a 40 by 40
  pixel sprite scaled up"; the first, finely drawn charge was rejected
  because 40 pixels could not hold it.

## Integration

**Chunk 1: Rifle, flashes and impacts.**

- The Rifle's frames replace the first pass's in place under the same keys;
  the first pass's manifest rows for them carry `superseded_by`.
- `ShotVfx` (`client/scripts/shot_vfx.gd`) is the one table of world shot
  pictures. `PlayerPawn.show_muzzle_flash` keeps each gun's size, timing and
  light, then shows the gun's own flash unmodulated, mirrored on alternate
  shots. Melee shows none.
- `ShotEffects` draws impact sprites on a second immediate surface,
  `Impacts`, with `shot_sprite.gdshader`: camera-facing quads 6 cm off the
  struck face and 8 cm toward the eye, frames advancing over the effect's
  0.24 s life, skipped whole when any corner is inside the camera clearance,
  and discarded by the shader if the eye later moves into one. The cream
  impact square is gone; the four tinted sparks stay for motion, and the
  melee dust quads stay under the melee sprite.
- Widths: wall puff 0.34 m, fighter hit 0.46 m, Railgun ring 0.6 m, melee
  0.28 m.

**Chunk 2: Level 8's Proximity Mine and Auditor (parked, ready).** Level 8's
custody devices reached `main` in [PR #323](https://github.com/blisspixel/fragr/pull/323)
while this pass ran, so the parked branch puts the art in through their seams:

- `WeaponArt.SUPPLY["proximity_mine"]` replaces the labelled crate through the
  existing pickup presenter. `WeaponArt.MINE_READY` and `MINE_PLACE` play in
  the grenade hand's slot: `GrenadeEffects.placed` reports a mine newly in
  flight, and the HUD's `show_mine_place` shows the held mine, then the toss,
  while the gun dips.
- `GrenadeEffects` lays the face-on device on the stuck surface (a
  `Sprite3D` on the surface normal over a thin dark body) and picks
  `WeaponArt.mine_device(phase, lit)` on every server tick, so the amber
  arming lamp and the red blink are the drawn lens.
- `EnemyView` loads `auditor.png` by kind; the Clerk placeholder, the 1.12
  scale and the box plate are gone, and the gold rim stays. The two repair
  lamps sit on the drawn plate's sockets in pawn space, at rest and raised
  while channeling, and `AuditorChannels` still lights one per repair left.
  `custody_frame` shows the atlas's channel cell.

**Chunk 3: Sabotage (parked, ready).** The Sabotage presentation is not on `main` yet; its
`SabotageArt` loads `res://assets/sabotage/<key>.png` by key when the file
exists, so `charge`, `plate_a`, `plate_b`, `prop_a` and `prop_b` replace its
placeholders with no code change. The new pictures need a line each where
that track wires them:

- Planted charge: draw `charge_planted.png` while armed and the beep lamp is
  on, `charge_planted_off.png` between beeps, `charge_planted_late.png` in the
  last seconds and `charge_planted_defused.png` after a defuse. They share one
  canvas, so the swap never moves the device.
- First person: `charge_carry.png` in the grenade hand's slot when the charge
  is picked up, `charge_plant.png` while planting and `charge_defuse.png`
  while defusing, each on the 241 by 180 weapon canvas with the gun dipped.
- Progress: `icon_plant.png` and `icon_defuse.png` beside the plant and defuse
  bar, 24 pixels, drawn at an integer scale.

**Chunk 4: the existing enemy roster.** No request was spent. The rig atlases
draw a standing body about 85 pixels tall in a 160 pixel cell, so a plate or
cloth panel is 5 to 15 pixels across; a generated texture or detail at that
size quantises to noise rather than readable form, and a generated body would
replace the rig, which this pass rules out. The roster's readability gains are
rig work: silhouettes, tells and the shader's existing one-texel outline. The
Auditor shows the route: a distinct silhouette (cap, coat, plate, spool) built
on the shared body, at no cost.

**Chunk 5: arena surfaces, parked.** The four procedural multiplayer arenas
(Directive 17 Substation, Sector 9 Transit Hall, Reclamation Gulch and
Tripoint Works) use the arcade steel shader alone, whose rust, hazard band and
red paint read as red and white noise. The parked work gives each a floor,
wall and cover tile, prepared like the 2026-10-01 Earth batch (128 square,
eight-pixel opposite-edge blend, final palette pass, `--verify` edge check),
mapped by `ArenaMaterials.ARENA_VENUES` through `EnvironmentTextures`
(`ARENA_TILES`, `assets/environment/arenas/`) at tile strength 0.8, with a new
`arcade_wear` shader switch, on by default, that drops the procedural vents,
rust, hazard band and paint for those venues and keeps seams, bevels, ribs and
bolts. One Sector 9 tour showed the first crate tile too loud; its grey
replacement and the other three arenas have not yet been seen in play.
