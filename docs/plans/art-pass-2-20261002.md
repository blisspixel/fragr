## Method

Unchanged from the first pass, which worked: edits of approved keepers with
the keeper as reference, a flat magenta `#FF00FF` field removed by
`reduce --key ff00ff`, palette lock to `docs/palette.json` in L\*a\*b\*, hard
alpha, PNG with image chunks only. New for this pass:

- **Flat colour fields.** Prompts for anything that reduces to small sizes ask
  for broad flat areas in the exact palette families (institutional
  grey-green, gunmetal, black steel) and no grain, speckle or weathering
  noise, because noise between two swatches is what made the Rifle blotchy.
- **Letters and lamp states are local.** Generators draw letters badly, and a
  lamp state must register exactly with its body. Site letters A and B and the
  charge and mine lamp states are composited in a GDScript bake over the
  generated body, so every state frame registers on the same canvas.
- **Effects are baked in GDScript.** Muzzle flashes and impacts are small,
  fast and must be palette exact; a deterministic bake (`tools/bake_shot_vfx.gd`)
  draws them in palette colours with a shape per weapon and frames per impact.
