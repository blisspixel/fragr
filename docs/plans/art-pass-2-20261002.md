## Method

Unchanged from the first pass, which worked: edits of approved keepers with
the keeper as reference, a flat magenta `#FF00FF` field removed by
`reduce --key ff00ff`, palette lock to `docs/palette.json` in L\*a\*b\*, hard
alpha, PNG with image chunks only. New for this pass:

- **Flat colour fields.** Prompts for anything that reduces to small sizes ask
  for broad flat areas in the exact palette families (institutional
  grey-green, gunmetal, black steel) and no grain, speckle or weathering
  noise, because noise between two swatches is what made the Rifle blotchy.
- **Letters and lamp states are local.** Generators draw letters badly, and a
  lamp state must register exactly with its body. Site letters A and B and the
  charge and mine lamp states are composited in a GDScript bake over the
  generated body, so every state frame registers on the same canvas.
- **Effects are baked in GDScript.** Muzzle flashes and impacts are small,
  fast and must be palette exact; a deterministic bake (`tools/bake_shot_vfx.gd`)
  draws them in palette colours with a shape per weapon and frames per impact.
- **Sub-palettes.** A picture is quantised to the subset of `docs/palette.json`
  its subject uses (`tools/spritegen/specs/palettes/`): free-side gear to
  bone, leather, ember and steel; Union issue to black steel, red and bone.
  Cream and tan shading otherwise lands on greens and cyans no part of the
  object has. A majority-vote downscale was tried and dropped: it kept
  outlines crisper but scattered outline pixels through flat areas.
- **Chunky subjects.** A pickup-sized subject is asked for as "a 40 by 40
  pixel sprite scaled up"; the first, finely drawn charge was rejected
  because 40 pixels could not hold it.

## Integration

**Chunk 1: Rifle, flashes and impacts.**

- The Rifle's frames replace the first pass's in place under the same keys;
  the first pass's manifest rows for them carry `superseded_by`.
- `ShotVfx` (`client/scripts/shot_vfx.gd`) is the one table of world shot
  pictures. `PlayerPawn.show_muzzle_flash` keeps each gun's size, timing and
  light, then shows the gun's own flash unmodulated, mirrored on alternate
  shots. Melee shows none.
- `ShotEffects` draws impact sprites on a second immediate surface,
  `Impacts`, with `shot_sprite.gdshader`: camera-facing quads 6 cm off the
  struck face and 8 cm toward the eye, frames advancing over the effect's
  0.24 s life, skipped whole when any corner is inside the camera clearance,
  and discarded by the shader if the eye later moves into one. The cream
  impact square is gone; the four tinted sparks stay for motion, and the
  melee dust quads stay under the melee sprite.
- Widths: wall puff 0.34 m, fighter hit 0.46 m, Railgun ring 0.6 m, melee
  0.28 m.
