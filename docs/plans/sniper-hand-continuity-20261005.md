# Sniper hand continuity

Status: planned, 2026-10-05. A bounded correction under the existing
[first-person quality plan](first-person-weapon-quality.md).

The selected Sniper first-person bake uses flat tan procedural gloves and a
broad tan sleeve. Other selected weapons use drawn rust-brown stitched leather
and dark cuffs. The same player must retain believable palm, finger and wrist
proportions across weapons while grip poses change naturally.

Use the accepted Shotgun drawing as the craft, glove and hand-scale reference,
and retain the Sniper's actual scoped civilian rifle identity: charcoal steel,
walnut stock, scope and practical retro-futurist form. Draw an idle on the
common 241x180 canvas and derive fire from that exact idle. Inspect grip,
scope/sight axis, visible wrists, pixel detail and settled rest at normal HUD
size. Reject generic rifle substitutions, giant hands or newly broken anatomy.

At most two idle candidates and two matching fire candidates may use the native
image pipeline. Price every stage before submission, cap combined prepaid use
at $0.50, retain all task receipts, and prohibit new cash or overages. No model
generation or rig is required. Historical sources and the world profile remain
retained. Scope, finite Cells, damage and resolved-shot timing stay server-owned.

Selection requires focused source/view/scope checks, an actual ordinary M07
Sniper discovery and firing witness, cross-weapon HUD comparison, final combined
CI and platform packages. Source geometry checks remain useful historical proof,
but do not approve these new drawn pictures automatically.
