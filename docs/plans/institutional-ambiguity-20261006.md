# Institutions, allegiance and responsibility

Status: **implemented**, guidance only, 2026-10-06.

## Goal and scope

Apply the current direction that the Union should feel like a plausible
institution populated by people, with the broader question of who deserves
trust becoming harder on reflection. Preserve readable combat allegiance,
conscious personhood, actual coercive harms and the free-side player viewpoint.
Keep the established ordinary lives, friendship and humor guidance.

Review the owning art, campaign, voice, cast, Office and Chancellery guidance.
Replace absolute villain behavior with credible duties, incentives, disagreements and
choices. Show consequences for free-side methods without forced moral symmetry,
a hidden morality score, new speech gates or hostility changes. Existing
recordings remain unchanged; new examples are authoring guidance rather than
new completed plot events.

Review the new M11 text against the same direction and reconcile Tern's brief
card-game reference with the cast's existing off-duty routine. No new paid
assets, performance claims, narrative branches or runtime authority are added.

## Result

The six owning guides now distinguish readable allegiance from individual
motives and judgment of methods. They retain the ordinary-lives direction and
specific coercive harms, while adding credible duties, institutional conflicts,
useful services and responsibility for free-side expedience. The Office and
Chancellery no longer assign one motive or unvarying emotional register to all
staff. The old claim that paperwork is always funnier than an atrocity is
replaced with humor grounded in a particular person's practical predicament.

M11's arrival, objective and physical panel text were reviewed. Its custody
labels describe actual functions; Tern's brief card-game line establishes a
personal concern without a faction speech. No runtime rewrite was needed.
The cast now records that line alongside the existing shipboard music routine.
Existing recorded audio, faction identification and combat hostility stay in
their established owners. Future authored scenes still need played review.

Changed prose passes `git diff --check` and the attribution and punctuation
scan. The referenced owning files and headings exist. No new implementation,
