## Behavior and ownership

Initial bounded constants follow the accepted weapon table: 18 body damage,
three server ticks between discharges (0.15 seconds), forty Cells per weapon
pickup, and one Cell spent for each actual discharge. The short and medium
role uses a 24 m finite ray, 0.02-radian spread and a preferred 6-18 m engagement
band. Reuse ordinary aimed fire, pitch, cover, body intersection, team rules,
spawn protection, death, records and feedback. Retain the existing head band
and traced head multiplier unless the shared design review explicitly changes
that choice. The visible discharge ends in a short settle rather than a long
Railgun beam or projectile flight. These are implemented foundation constants,
pending ordinary played balance and listening acceptance.

Arc damage bypasses both carried armor and registered frontal armor plates.
It never bypasses solid cover, friendly-fire rules, invulnerability or companion
protection. Armor is left intact while HP receives the resolved damage; effective
damage and kills use the shared authoritative ledger. Expose a weapon property
for plate owners rather than teaching an Assessor a second damage path. Root
owns the new Assessor and M12 plate rules; coordinate the existing shield helper
before either lane edits it. The Arc lane owns its ordinary hit-resolution armor
branch. Every shot remains a normal resolved shot with its own weapon identity.

The canonical M12 Heavy lesson uses a bounded optional authored armor count,
defaulting to zero, accepted only for Heavy Sweeper placements and capped at 100.
That actual carried armor enters the existing server damage and record path.
This lane owns its strict placement validation and spawn assignment. Existing
Heavy placements retain zero armor and their established finite-supply balance;
the new lesson explicitly places 100 armor on its Heavy. The Assessor's separate
registered plate remains owned by its encounter implementation.

A joined human loads twelve Cells into the Arc's capacitor magazine and uses
the existing rising-edge R policy, taking twenty-two ticks (1.1 seconds) to
refill. The magazine is part of the same finite Cells total shared with Railgun
and Sniper. First discovery fills from the pickup's actual rounds, duplicates
refill only the bag, and dry fire does not switch weapons. Agents and campaign
enemies keep their established finite single pool. Reload cancellation, death,
retry, carry and input sequence rules remain shared.
