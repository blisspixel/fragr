## Observed problem and source examples

isolated treads, open landing edges or unsupported gallery slabs read as built
architecture. Give every flight an architectural purpose, supported sides,
clear entrances and protected landings. Interior stairs normally belong in a
stairwell. An exposed industrial flight needs an intentional supported and
guarded structure.
