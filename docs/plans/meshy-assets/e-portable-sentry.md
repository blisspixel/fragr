## Lore, use and design

Use: M20 Local Exception and the shared Wipe multiplayer scenario, planned.

A foldable civilian-built tripod with a compact practical gun cradle, visible
ammunition box and mechanical slew joints. Repaired steel, warm paint and
individual work marks distinguish it from Union fixed Rail turrets. Its broad
angular silhouette must read at playing resolution against the district's
waterworks materials. Keep practical circa-2070 pixel surfaces and physical
controls, without decorative holograms or photographic texture noise.

Owners: [Wipe design](../wipe-survival.md), [Local Exception](../../campaign/l20-local-exception.md),
[people and agents](../../lore/people-and-agents.md), [art bible](../../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md)
and [palette](../../palette.json). This is hardwired automatic equipment, not
a conscious captive or a free agent forced into a gun emplacement. Its locally
isolated control and targeting hardware must be established in the owning
gameplay contract; repainting networked Union equipment cannot defeat takeover.
