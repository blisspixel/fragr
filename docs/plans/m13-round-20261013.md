## Implemented

decoration. Requests now use the same facing, eye reach and unobstructed line
of sight check as other mission controls, against the current collision world.
Foundry release, relay and hazard effects share living contact eligibility:
detached, eliminated, dead and respawn-waiting bodies cannot operate them.

The encounter lifecycle now restores foundry progress when the last participant
leaves or dies. The deck returns to its parked height; relay, worker choices
and actual hazard-damage receipts reset with encounters and supplies. Ticks
remain monotonic. The lift's collision sweep still refuses every rider/world
obstruction as a whole step, while unrelated overlapping bystanders elsewhere
no longer block the deck. Invalid body heights fail closed.

No map bytes, mission identity, wire, save, client or menu contract changed.
The three worker responses remain separate facts and optional release never
becomes an exit key. The protected town feed remains immune to the relay shot.
