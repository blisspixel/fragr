# Passenger Manifest berth structure

Status: In flight. Scope approved 2026-10-04.

The original M09 prototype has a clean 27-state ordinary-input completion
baseline at source `42c00dcc`: all 21 guards, seven Enforcers, crew release,
eight ordered fights and fresh departure passed with zero deaths and dry
triggers. This establishes gameplay operation, not final architectural quality.
The large lunar air volume is intentional. It must read as a working impound
hangar, with useful wide fights and service circulation, rather than an empty
arena. Office density is not the objective of this pass.

## Bounded change

Keep the 100 m outer bounds, 26 m pressure roof, original fight widths, guard
starts, supplies, eight encounter order, single boarding door, console targets,
crew routes and departure ownership. Add a covered cargo receiving threshold
and supported stair canopy at the entry. Make the long service galleries actual
raised slabs with registered structural columns, retaining their exact upper
surfaces. Add open-sided service shelters and overhead hangar ribs so scale and
purpose read from the route. Connect the pressure cabin to a stepped stern
superstructure and supported hull cradle. No new objective or paid source.

Every blocking slab, beam, roof and column belongs in authoritative map solids.
The client may add registered surface markings and nonblocking carrier detail;
it cannot close a route with a decorative mesh. The existing office and crew
contact semantics remain intact. No cosmetic giant ship is allowed to hide
collision mismatches. A final detailed ship source remains separate art work.

## Acceptance

- Load both prepared hatch worlds with the strict schema. Prove supports and
  headroom at the existing player and crew feet using shared movement.
- Preserve all 21 bodies, seven Enforcers, ordered triggers, supply claims and
  single existing boarding door. Test the same actual idle/low-resource entry.
- Run actual controller/contact preflight for all four Edda/Splice rosters,
  including held crew, release, every supplied route and optional boarding.
- Repeat the complete 27-state ordinary-input combat route on a matching
  native build. Retain every rejected route and exact source/binary hash.
- Inspect entry threshold, gantry underside/supports, carrier/cradle, upper
  shelter and departure from actual rendered views. Do not infer hardware
  performance or fresh human fun from one scripted tour.

Risks: hollowed slabs expose lower circulation and can change navigation
choices; new columns can snag bodies; canopy edges can block raised shots or
hover volumes. Keep original authored destinations and prove those interactions
before a renderer rerun. The original route ended at 70 HP with 22 Cells,
three grenades and one mine, but automated finite-supply collection is not
proof that a fresh player finds that pacing fair. Real par remains unauthored.
