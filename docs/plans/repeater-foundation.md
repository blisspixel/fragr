## Candidate behavior

Use the existing `Action.fire` hold and discrete weapon selection. Append
`repeater` after the seven existing weapon identities. Never change an earlier
index or call its damage Rifle damage. Preserve the default arcade kit.

Initial feel-candidate constants: six warmup ticks (0.30 seconds), two ticks
between shots (ten per second), 14 direct damage, 35 m reach and 0.035-radian
spread. Reuse ordinary ray resolution, cover, armor and statistics. These are
prototype values, not a final balance claim or altered existing enemy timing.
Spend one Bullet only for each actual shot. Warmup spends none. Release stops
the stream and resets warmup; weapon switch, inactivity, death, leave and
attempt reset also clear it. An empty pool produces no damage or successful
shot. Ordinary finite inventory caps and dry-trigger accounting remain intact.

The server owns the private cycle. No new warmup wire fact or presenter is added
in this CPU foundation, as approved on 2026-10-04. A later actual weapon
presentation needs its own strict fact and acceptance gate. The client cannot infer
hits or successful fire from warmup/trigger hold. Test the shared equipment
controller against a genuine ranged target and the finite pool; no MCP hot path.
