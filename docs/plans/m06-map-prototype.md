The lane bends east through loading, then reconnects to central customs and its western service branch. Two galleries at height 3 use six 0.5-metre rises, broad treads and clear headroom. The west Turret initially faces east; real cover breaks its charge and the west gallery supplies a rear flank. Recoveries and observation positions stay outside following encounter thresholds.

Required groups are `freight_ingress` (two Clerks, two Sweepers), `rail_lane` (one Sweeper), `loading_heavy` (one Heavy), `turret_intro` (one Turret), `customs_hold` (four Clerks, three Sweepers), and `exit_watch` (two Clerks, one Heavy, one Turret). Optional `service_branch` has two Clerks and one Sweeper after loading. It never becomes a required predecessor. The first Turret belongs to the customs eight-guard composition; the exit adds the second. Total: 18 required and three optional.

Reuse the core lane's strict `m06` geometry: six Arrival objectives, optional service marker, companion start, and registered departure panel/approach/boarding. Required IDs are `freight_cleared`, `rail_lane_cleared`, `loading_cleared`, `turret_cleared`, `customs_cleared`, `exit_cleared`, then `party_departed`. Core owns schema, staging, registered decoration kinds and runtime; client owns presentation and shared QA callers; root owns durable carry, refill, shared documentation and checks. This lane owns the JSON map, route tests, ordinary-input QA manifest and bounded temporal Turret observer with its pure harness.

Three secret locations provide pressure-maintenance armor/Cells, crane-overlook armor with a customs angle, and duty-free Shells/medkit. Exactly one pickup at each location carries the secret flag. Found Railgun stock plus a separate ten-Cell pickup establishes the lesson. Other finite supplies support ordinary misses and recovery; a full-health medkit may remain unclaimed. No supplies overlap entry pickup reach. No grants, teleports, forced enemy phases, timing or damage changes in the route.
