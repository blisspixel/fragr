## Goal and room composition

weighing and inspection gantry occupies the unused southern freight apron, with
two clamped shipments on one platform. The two existing customs desks receive
issued display assemblies. A personal-luggage inspection bed and a document
sorter occupy opposite customs edges. These are a small composition of actual
activities, not a scatter of generic crates or cosmetic cover.

practicals preserve lunar-port variety. Black/red is reserved for issued equipment
and small security seals; it is primarily the Union outfit identity. Angular
forms, pixel materials and simple fictional display icons retain the stylized
circa 2070 house look. Static machinery implies no conveyor, pressure puzzle,
new interaction, grant or progression requirement.
