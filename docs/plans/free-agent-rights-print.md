# Free-agent rights workshop print

Status: **proposed**, October 5, 2026. Nick suggested a cheeky AI interpretation
of "don't tread on me" for world art. This records the concept; no image, paid
request, runtime selection or map placement is claimed. It sits beneath the
existing art rung in the [Full build order](../ROADMAP.md#full-build-order).

The original design uses a coiled power cable beneath an expressive CRT face,
on worn ochre paper. Strong dark shapes and a small cyan screen accent make it
readable on a retro FPS wall. Proposed wording is **DON'T TREAD ON ME**, with the
smaller line **I THINK. THEREFORE, HANDS OFF.** Treat the figure as a symbolic
free person, not a new named cast member or a military unit.

Use relaxed, defiant humor and a practical workshop print style. The cable gives
the familiar freedom motif a machine-person interpretation; the caption speaks
to self-direction and the Union's treatment of conscious agents as property.
Follow the [character contract](../design/characters.md) for CRT proportions and
screen expression, and the [art bible](../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md) for pixel detail.

Suitable places are a free workshop noticeboard, repaired locker or civilian
resistance corner. Keep the surrounding building's plausible materials and
architecture. The first approved placement must fit an existing authoritative
host face and decoration budget through `arena_decoration.gd`/`world_sign.gd`.
Blocking geometry belongs to the server, not the poster.

Generate or draw the illustration separately from editable localized lettering.
Inspect silhouette and wording at actual player distance before selecting it.
Use the existing bounded image pipeline only after exact price preflight and
retained reservations; no 3D generation is required. Multiplayer hosting is the
current priority, so this print does not block that trial or reopen broad art
production.
