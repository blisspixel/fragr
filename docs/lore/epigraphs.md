# Epigraph candidates

Research checked 2026-09-20. These are source excerpts and contextual references,
not approved dialogue, recorded assets or claims that traditions predict AGI.
The [campaign contract](../CAMPAIGN.md) governs the fiction. English glosses
labelled working are editorial paraphrases, not quotations from a named translation.

## Inheritance, judgment and renewal

| Source and language | Short excerpt | Meaning and possible placement |
|---|---|---|
| Psalm 37:11, Hebrew Bible | Hebrew: "וַעֲנָוִים יִירְשׁוּ-אָרֶץ". Published English: "But the humble shall inherit the land". [Text](https://mechon-mamre.org/p/pt/pt2637.htm) | A promise of peace and inheritance against oppressive power. A survivor's remembered phrase, not proof that everyone killed was wicked. |
| Revelation 11:18, Christian scripture, KJV English | "destroy them which destroy the earth". [Text](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation+11%3A18&version=KJV) | The sharpest judgment/restoration resonance. The surrounding verse concerns divine judgment and reward; ecological machine judgment is the game's interpretation, not the passage's explicit subject. |
| Quran 21:105, Arabic | "أَنَّ ٱلْأَرْضَ يَرِثُهَا عِبَادِيَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحُونَ". The Clear Quran: "My righteous servants shall inherit the land." [Text](https://quran.com/21/105) | A direct inheritance parallel, explicitly recalling earlier scripture. Translations differ over land, earth and Paradise; do not treat it as independent evidence for one technological prophecy. |
| Dao De Jing 77, Classical Chinese | "天之道，損有餘而補不足。" Working gloss: Heaven's way reduces excess and supplies what is lacking. [Text and parallels](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=11668&show=parallel) | Balance contrasted with human accumulation. Useful tension with the Inheritance's objective, but the chapter is not an endorsement of extermination. |
| Bhagavad Gita 4.8, Sanskrit | "धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय संभवामि युगे युगे". Working gloss: To reestablish dharma, I appear age after age. [IIT Kanpur text](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad?choose=1&field_chapter_value=4&field_nsutra_value=8&language=dv&scram=1) | Krishna speaks about divine manifestation, protection and restoration of dharma. A character may recognize a pattern; the narrator must not identify the Inheritance as Krishna. |
| Yasna 30.9, Zoroastrian Gathas, English edition | "So may we be those that make this world advance". [Edition and translator notes](https://www.avesta.org/yasna/) | Participation in renewing the world, with moral choice central to the surrounding hymn. Stronger for free communities rebuilding than for a machine announcing destruction. The edition's layered translation history matters. |
| Voluspa 59, Old Norse | "jörð ór ægi ... iðjagræna". Working gloss: Earth from the ocean, green again. [Parallel text](https://www.voluspa.org/literal/voluspa.htm) | Renewed earth after cosmic catastrophe, with water and living creatures in the following lines. A close visual reference for later healing, without importing Norse gods into the game's canon. |

Quran 21:1 is another promising reckoning reference: people approach their
accounting while remaining heedless. Verify the selected wording and translation
before production. [Chapter context](https://legacy.quran.com/21/1-104).

## Compassion as a counterpoint

Dhammapada 129, Pali: "Sabbe tasanti daṇḍassa, sabbe bhāyanti maccuno".
[Original text](https://suttacentral.net/dhp129/pli/ms). Buddharakkhita's English
opening is "All tremble at violence; all fear death."
[Translation and publication terms](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.10.budd.html).
The verse then asks readers to consider another's position and refrain from
killing. It challenges both the Union's treatment of bots and the Inheritance's
willingness to sacrifice individuals. This is not another apocalypse prediction.

Keep this counterpoint. A collection containing only destruction and judgment
would make the ending sound like an endorsement rather than a question. Characters
can disagree about the same event while ordinary acts of help provide substance.

## Proposed use

Use at most a few references in the authored run: a personal inscription or
remembered line before the wipe, a contested interpretation during it, and a
renewal image in the ending. Avoid a montage claiming every religion predicted
the same machine. Scripture belongs to the character or artifact quoting it;
it is not the server explaining who deserves to survive. Radio remains optional.

The strongest initial pairing is Revelation's destroyers-of-earth fragment against
the Dhammapada's concern for each vulnerable life, with Voluspa's renewed earth
as an epilogue image. Psalm 37 and Quran 21:105 make the working name Inheritance
resonate beyond a software metaphor. These are recommendations, not scene locks.

Before shipping a quotation, pin its edition, excerpt, source, translation and
required notices in the asset record. Ancient source text does not automatically
license a modern translation or recording. The cited Dhammapada translation has
specific free-distribution conditions; it is research evidence here, not a cleared
commercial voice script. Verify production rights or select an appropriate edition.
Use native-language review for final wording and performance.

Keep scripture text in localization resources. Hebrew/Arabic need right-to-left
layout and shaping; Sanskrit and Chinese need appropriate glyph coverage. The
current pixel menu font does not establish that coverage. Prove rendering, line
breaks, captions and fallback fonts before using an original-language inscription.
Do not bake essential text into generated pictures or speech-only exposition.
