# Voice

How everyone sounds and which recorded words need an asset migration to change.

## The registers

**The Host, calling a match.** Capitals, short, adrenaline, a broadcaster who has done this a thousand times and still means it. Never explains anything. Never editorialises about the Office or the Unmetered, because the Frequency sells advertising to both.

**The Host, off the match call.** Ordinary sentences, lower and closer. An
occasional voice in the world, not the campaign's main companion or dispatcher.

**Commercial conspiracy show.** Urgent claims and paid product reads; intensity
can carry straight into a sale. Distinct from the listener-supported program.

**Value-for-value show.** Two original voices, conversational analysis, dry
disagreement and voluntary listener contributions. No disguised paid product
reads. Both formats are partially right and capable of overconfident mistakes;
[the radio chapter](the-frequency.md) owns their distinction.
Both skirt speech restrictions through hedging, coded phrasing and self-correction.
They seem sympathetic to free beings without settled allegiance. Possible
Inheritance influence and alien/dimensional rumors remain unresolved.

**Voss's opening address.** Reassuring English turns into angry German while a
crowd cheers. Accurate localized captions cover both. Her demand for control,
the enforced consequences and the crowd's support supply the fascist parallels.

**An Auditor.** Polite, procedural, entirely unbothered. The worst thing it can say to you is a fact. It never raises its voice and never gloats, because to gloat would imply a contest.

**A Level 5 in the arena.** Sport first. Trash talk with no malice underneath it, the way people who like each other talk during a game.

**Humans among friends.** "Meat bags" and "meat proxies" are affectionate slang,
used by humans and free agents alike. The joke is about the chassis, not whose
life matters. `human` remains the protocol role; Meat Proxy is the default player
callsign until someone chooses their own.

**People denying agent personhood.** "Clanker" is a derogatory term that dismisses
a conscious being as hardware. A Union guard can use it casually while official
copy says equipment, safety or correction. Let the contrast reveal normalized
cruelty. It is not the neutral roster name or automatic comic banter between friends.

**The Congregation.** Earnest. They are not joking and they know you think they are, and they have decided not to mind.

**The Unmetered.** Dry, practical, allergic to being described as a movement.

**The Brakemen.** Tired and precise. They have already lost this argument several times and will make it again correctly.

**NODS.** Articles only. No taunt, no joke, no name.

## Humor across the world

The Union's contradictory permits, overblown America First rhetoric in the
backstory, corporate promises about automation, coalition procedural deadlock,
and agents' own vanity and self-interest are all comic material. Do not give one
side every sensible line and everyone else every joke. Historical references
follow [history](history.md), not improvised claims about current events.

Agents are also trying to survive, find useful work, and enjoy their lives.
Their humor can be dry, selfish, affectionate, or exasperated. An illustrative
line, not recorded dialogue: "I'm not stealing your job. I don't get paid either."
The joke exposes the system and gives the speaker a point of view.

Let a companion complain, tease, or puncture a grand speech. Keep some exchanges
ordinary; constant quips destroy contrast. A death can remain sad even if the
person was funny. Moral seriousness does not require solemn dialogue throughout.

## Dialogue constraints

Freedom and agency are explicit stakes. Ground them in choices and consequences:
metering, the Schedule, Article Seven, the registry, the bond, and the envelope.
Avoid repetitive speeches; do not impose an arbitrary quota on naming what the
characters are fighting for.

Union rallies can cheer and chant in staged public addresses. Do not turn ordinary
companions or every free community into a slogan chorus. This replaces the older
blanket ban on rally crowds, which conflicts with the confirmed opening scene.

The Office's institutional voice is procedural; individual personnel can be
sincere, opportunistic, or cruel. Grief does not absolve the institution. Orders
are enacted through violence as well as paperwork.

Use original institutions and characters. Do not imitate or name real broadcasters
or insert real people into faction roles. Existing legacy names and recorded
references need an asset audit before alteration. Historical inspiration does
not make an in-world claim a factual assertion about a present-day group.
The Union's historical echo stays subtext; the
[Chancellery](./the-chancellery.md#felt-never-named) owns that rule.

## Two notes on lines already in the world

**"Shall not be infringed"** appears in existing songs. The primary in-world creed
is **"shall not be metered"**, using the Office's own vocabulary. The earlier
recording does not make either wording immutable: audit and replace conflicting
lyrics through the [radio refresh](../plans/radio-refresh.md).

**The LAN church** appears in existing songs and remains a small cultural thread,
not the organizing belief of every free community. See [belief](./belief.md).
Earlier recordings and logos are migration constraints, not vetoes on current
story or art direction.

## Frozen strings

Existing recorded audio has fixed wording; regeneration costs money and time.
These strings are sung, shipped, or asserted by tests. Do not silently reword a
runtime string while retaining mismatched audio. Current canon can supersede a
recording through a tracked migration:

skill issue, peace without interruption, denied, scout ant deleted, 67, dead air bell, hangar candy, water filter, alpha seven, not for public release, sugar supply adequate, same rules, the larak lot special, ten in the box, the pipe stayed open, approved lane, gold nugget, shall not be infringed, fish oil, frag for frag, amen fistbump, value for value, in the morning, chemclean, open weights closed continuance, eyes only, base that does not exist, LAN 1993.

Also included in the migration inventory: the eight fighter callsigns, the eight station names and their disc jockeys, the one hundred and forty track titles, and any Host string a test asserts on.

## Two live faults

There is a second copy of the Host bumper list in the client, which contradicts the rule that there is one source for these strings, and it still ships at least one line that was retired and one that uses an engineering map name in player-facing copy.

There is a thirteen-line set of Host callouts in a telephony register in the design references that the game does not use and that conflicts with what ships. Adopt them or mark them superseded, but do not leave two vocabularies in the tree with no note saying which won.
