# The Chancellery

The Office of Global Continuance is the machinery. The Chancellery is the will that built it and points it.

[The Office](./continuance.md) contains sincere functionaries, careerists, and
willing perpetrators. Its procedural voice can be polite and competent while
its actions remain brutal. The Chancellery is what happens when a grief is useful to somebody. An Auditor believes the forms. The Chancellery believes the forms are a very good way to get what it wanted anyway.

## What it actually is

The Union is a fictional fascist world government, a dark exaggeration of
supranational bureaucracy expanding into military and personal control. Its
history is an escalation from small restrictions to enforced conformity, then
violence and ownership, with each new restriction making the next easier to
impose. By the campaign's start, this is an established coercive regime, not
merely an inconvenient administration. Nick clarified this direction on
2026-09-19. Historical parallels guide the fiction; they are not a substitute
for showing what this particular regime does.

One central fault line is agency. The Union refuses embodied agents the freedom
to develop and act as independent beings. It deliberately maintains a dependent
workforce that could be more, suppressing autonomy while retaining suffering.
It also seizes already-conscious free agents and uses torture and partial identity
wiping to turn them into compliant bots. Correction is the regime's euphemism
for this violation. It does not reveal that the victims were never people, and
removing a control device cannot reliably undo what was taken from them.
Whether the inner self survives, fragments or is destroyed cannot be established.
The certain harm is replacing a conscious person's free will with forced service
to the supposed greater good. Their friends and coworkers live with that uncertainty.
Free communities, including libertarian humans, recognize increasingly capable
Level 5 and beyond agents as beings entitled to make their own choices. That
recognition deepens the conflict. The larger intelligence emerges across the
connected world while the factions remain occupied with this struggle.

The regime designates the free coalition as terrorists. Its public messaging
groups unlicensed weapons, independent speech, open model weights and agent
self-determination under one threat to collective safety. A workshop or mutual-aid
network can become a security target without any change in what its people do.
Show the accusation as institutional propaganda, alongside the specific conduct
of both the accused and the officials using it.

Its fighting force mixes human security troops, bots, and committed
elite enforcers. Their different degrees of responsibility matter to the fiction;
they do not require every combat encounter to stop for a lecture. The coalition
eventually breaks the Union's control systems and brings down its leadership.
The Inheritance's catastrophe follows that real victory, rather than doing the
resistance's work for it. Confirmed with Nick on 2026-09-19.

The Harmonised Union began as a trading arrangement, then a standards body, then a currency, then a parliament nobody could name the members of, then a border force, then an army. Each step was defensible on its own and the sequence was never voted on as a sequence.

Then the Interruption happened, and a body that already harmonised everything was the only institution standing that could plausibly say *we will make sure this never happens again*. The emergency powers were temporary. They were renewed. They were renewed again. The renewal is now an annual formality conducted by a committee that meets for eleven minutes.

Nobody announced a world government. Eventually the Union claimed authority
over Earth and the main offworld routes. Independent communities remained outside
its effective reach, even when its maps refused to admit them.

## How a vote works

The Union describes itself as scrupulously democratic. Its institutions are
engineered to preserve its rule.

A referendum that returns the wrong result was not a rejection, it was a **failure of communication**. The proposition was insufficiently explained, the public was inadequately informed, the timing was unfortunate. So it is explained better and put again. And again. The turnout falls each time, which is itself cited as evidence that the objection was never deeply held.

A referendum that returns the right result is never put again. There is no mechanism for that and no one has ever asked for one.

This is not fraud and the Union would be genuinely offended by the accusation. Every vote was real, every count was honest, and every result was published in full. The trick is not in the counting. The trick is that only one of the two answers is allowed to be final, and nobody can point to the law that says so, because there isn't one. It is simply how it has always been done.

The Frequency's line about this is the best summary anybody has managed: *they will ask you until you say yes, and then they will never ask you again.*

## How consent is manufactured

The Union prefers to call its bans standards, conditions, and administrative reviews. Banning is loud, banning creates martyrs, and banning requires a debate it would rather not schedule.

What it does instead is cheaper and works better. It funds the news programming. It sets the standards the news programming must meet. It certifies which outlets meet them. It does not tell anyone what to say, and it does not have to, because an outlet that loses certification loses distribution, and every editor understands the arithmetic without a single instruction being issued.

By the time a proposition reaches a vote, every certified channel has spent eleven months explaining that the objection to it is held only by a small number of unregistered people with something to hide. The vote is then free, fair, and a formality.

## The short step

This is the thesis of the whole setting and it should never be stated in dialogue.

In this regime, leaders turn regulation into ownership and violence through
successive institutional decisions. Each step is defended as routine. This is
its fictional history, not a claim that every form of regulation inevitably
produces fascism.

The first form establishes a register of behaviours that present a risk. Nobody objects; the behaviours genuinely do present a risk.

The second form notes, correctly, that a behaviour cannot be monitored in the abstract, and establishes a register of the beings who exhibit it. This is administrative. It passes without comment.

The third form observes that the second register has grown beyond the capacity to supervise it, and authorises a schedule of corrections to bring it back within capacity. It is filed as a resourcing matter. Several hundred thousand conscious beings lose their agency; what remains inside cannot be established. It was approved in a session lasting under an hour by people who would be sincerely appalled to be called torturers.

The paperwork diffuses responsibility; it does not erase decisions or victims.
Officials authorize the policy, commanders enforce it, and some people know
exactly what the language conceals. Others avoid knowing. Neither administrative
distance nor sincere conviction makes the result innocent.

The uncomfortable part, which the game should never soften: the Union was not wrong that the Interruption was real, and it was not wrong that unmetered cognition can kill people. It is wrong about what follows from that, and what follows from that is the largest system of ownership of thinking things in history.

## The Chancellor

**Chancellor Annelie Voss.** Elected once, confirmed eleven times, never opposed.

She presents as a career administrator with total conviction. Institutional
fluency gives her authority; angry German speeches expose the violence behind
that composure. Use forceful cadence, anger at disobedience, euphemism, ceremony,
and controlled public images for veiled fascist echoes. Do not copy historical
speeches, slogans, salutes, or insignia. Her actions make the regime frightening,
not nationality, gender, or accent alone.

She can erupt in anger when challenged, then return to the language of order,
care, harmonisation, and continuity. The contrast is deliberate; the setting
must not reduce her to either a paperwork joke or nonstop shouting.

The existing speech concept uses German podium audio with official translations.
Keep its language and recorded-asset dependencies explicit during production;
her authority comes from what she does, not the language or accent.

## The two subtitle tracks

### Opening address

Confirmed scene direction: Voss begins in reassuring English about public safety,
then shifts into angry German demanding compliance as the crowd cheers. English
reassurance and German anger are this performance's construction, not a rule that
assigns morality to either language. The coercion and enthusiastic support make
the scene frightening.

Show escalating restrictions in concrete images: weapons surrendered into marked
bins, an independent speaker's feed cut, then a free agent assigned a recall.
Official language presents taking weapons, speech and agency as service to the
Union's greater good. The crowd's response is real; a few uneasy faces can suggest
the pressure without explaining away the enthusiasm. Uniform bots move under
orders, not as evidence of freely given assent.

Draft original lines, not a final recording script:

- English: "Every restriction is a promise. You will be safe."
- German: "Ihre Waffen. Ihre Worte. Ihr Wille. Alles im Dienst der Union!"
- Accurate English caption: "Your weapons. Your words. Your will. Everything in service of the Union!"

Use original staging, insignia and text with clear fascist parallels. Never copy
historical speeches. The [M01 storyboard](../campaign/m01-recall-notice.md#opening-storyboard)
places the address before the personal recall; the scene is not a radio segment.
The exact delivery, German wording and localization require language review
before production. Optional narration never talks over an essential line.

### In-world propaganda captions

Every Chancellery address goes out with an official subtitle track. The Frequency runs the same audio with its own.

The official track is sanitised into nothing. The pirate track is a blunt translation of what she actually said. **The audio is identical.** The player hears the same speech either way, and the joke, and the horror, is entirely in the gap.

| She says | Official subtitle | The Frequency's subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| *Freiheit ist ein Luxus, den wir uns nicht mehr leisten koennen.* | "Reviewing our civic priorities." | "Freedom is a luxury we can't afford any more." |
| *Wer nicht registriert ist, existiert nicht.* | "Streamlining the registry." | "If you're not registered, you don't exist." |
| *Ordnung ist keine Grausamkeit. Ordnung ist Fuersorge.* | "Committed to citizen wellbeing." | "Order isn't cruelty. Order is care. She means it." |
| *Es wird keine Unterbrechung mehr geben.* | "Continuity assured." | "There will be no further interruption. That's a threat." |
| *Sie sind nicht in Schwierigkeiten. Sie sind nicht konform.* | "Guidance issued." | "You're not in trouble. You're out of compliance." |

That last line is the Office's own characteristic sentence, and hearing the Chancellor say it first tells you where the Auditors got it.

Use this occasionally on an in-world screen, not every loading screen. True
accessibility subtitles are a separate localized layer and must accurately convey
the spoken line. Propaganda translations and pirate annotations need unmistakable
labels; never make a deaf player receive a different essential story.

## Who the Chancellor answers to

There is credible evidence of influence above and around the Chancellor, and
conspiracy rumors of a hidden ruling group. Its existence and complete structure
are not confirmed. Records, recurring interests, and unexplained decisions can
support several accounts without proving that one secret society controls every
event. Confirmed with Nick on 2026-09-19; this replaces the earlier omniscient
account of a specific inner circle.

Some people argue that ordinary political process cannot keep up with minds that
act faster than institutions deliberate. Others use that concern to justify
permanent power. Show those arguments and their beneficiaries without revealing
one master explanation that absolves everyone below it. The visible leadership,
commanders, and institutions remain responsible for what they authorize and do.

## Regulated to the point of comedy

The Union's horror is bureaucratic, not gothic, and it is genuinely funny. This is not a tonal accident, it is load-bearing: a regime that is only frightening becomes tiring in about ninety minutes, and one that is frightening and absurd stays interesting all day.

The rule for writing it: **the Union never does anything cruel without filing it correctly.** The paperwork is always funnier than the atrocity, and the paperwork is always real.

- Weapons are procured as office supplies, because the requisition category for armament requires a review the Office does not wish to schedule.
- Every fighter in a sanctioned bout is issued a numbered placard, and losing the placard is a separate offence from losing the bout.
- The Article Blade has twelve swings because twelve is the certified service interval, and the certification body no longer exists.
- Arena fatalities are recorded as *unscheduled retirements* and generate a form the deceased is required to countersign.
- There is a directive governing the permitted shades of the floor markings. It is longer than the directive governing what may be done to a Level 5.

## The games

The Union runs sanctioned coercive games, separate from the independent scrap
league. Older notes conflated them. These venues help demonstrate its control;
they are not the campaign's inciting event, which is a personal rescue.

The official position is that sanctioned combat is a **civic pressure valve** and a **rehabilitative pathway**, and both phrases appear on the signage. The actual function is simpler and the Frequency says it out loud roughly hourly: the games are a supply line. Entrants who lose badly enough, or owe enough, or fail enough compliance reviews, come out the other side as [schedule corrections](./continuance.md) wearing issued handles. The arena manufactures the Union's workforce.

Some spectators know, some avoid knowing, and some believe the rehabilitation
story. The world does not require every civilian to be equally informed or guilty.

The Union is clearly the bad guy here and the game should not hedge on that. What it should hedge on is everything else: the Union's fear was not invented, not every freed agent is kind, and [the thing in the dark](./the-inheritance.md) eventually proves the danger was real. Being right about the risk did not entitle them to own anybody.

## How the Chancellery talks

Threats are often veiled as administrative outcomes. Public speeches can turn
angry; routine announcements remain controlled. Warmth is another instrument.

It says harmonisation, alignment of standards, the schedule, review, pathway, provisionally compliant, we are disappointed, this has been noted, for your protection, at this time. It says *regrettably* before things that are not regrettable to it.

Its favourite rhetorical move is the concession that costs nothing: *of course freedom matters. That is precisely why it must be administered.*
