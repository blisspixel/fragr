# Level 16: Freedom of Movement

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. The first half of [M09](m09-peace-without-interruption.md) in the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md): the motorcycle run to
the Forever Office. [Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Par | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IV Reckoning | The ceremonial avenue, stadium to Office | Motorcycle | 11 min | 4:00 | 0 |

**Premise.** The stadium lit the fuse. Regional uprisings are breaking
enforcement from the edges in, and the coalition needs a foothold at the
Forever Office before dawn. Between the stadium and the Office runs the
ceremonial avenue the Union built for its parades. You ride it.

**Hook.** A night motorcycle charge down the regime's own parade route, lights
turning green ahead of you, the Office tower growing the whole way.

**Teaches.** The motorcycle. The stadium's ceremonial gate opens onto a plaza
with one bike and twenty seconds of empty road: walk up, press Use, ride. A
toppled barricade across the first stretch is the first hop. The rider does not
shoot in this level; the bike is movement, and dodging a Notary's committed
burst by weaving is the verb. Lose the bike and the transit station is the foot
route, with its own fights.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The ceremonial gate, the plaza, the bike. The Office tower at
   the far end of the avenue, lit.
2. **First fight.** Not a fight: the first stretch, a barricade hop and the
   first Notary flash, dodged.
3. **Escalation.** Checkpoints: Clerks behind barriers, a Turret on a parade
   stand, Notary patrols overhead. The avenue splits into side alleys around
   each checkpoint; the alleys are faster and narrower.
4. **Set piece.** The green wave. Every traffic light down the avenue turns
   green ahead of the bikes, one after another, and the checkpoint gates lift
   on their own schedule. Mara, on the channel: "That wasn't us." Nobody
   answers.
5. **Breath.** The transit route checkpoint: a prison bus with people inside
   and two Clerks. Optional, and quick.
6. **Turn.** Behind you, the avenue fills. The parade route the Union built to
   march on carries the people coming for it.
7. **Climax.** The forecourt, dismounted: two attacks on the Office entrance,
   allies holding the left flank. Enforcers down the entrance steps, Turrets in
   the portico, a Heavy Sweeper, an Assessor over the fountain.
8. **Exit.** The foothold at the Office's chamber doors.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The Office tower at the end of the avenue,
growing the whole run. The traffic lights, a line of signals that becomes the
level's event. The forecourt fountain, the one landmark you stop at.

**Doors.** None. The Office's chamber doors are level 17's first door.

**Secrets.**
- A coalition cache behind the civic fountain, the six painted on a coin
  drain: armor and Rockets.
- A rooftop jump line over a parade grandstand, readable from the avenue by a
  ramp of stacked risers, a six on the landing: Cells and a medkit.
- The *Spontaneous celebration zone* kiosk, a six on its shutter: Shells and
  Bullets.

**Brief.** Assisted: free the checkpoint prisoners on the transit route.
Standard adds: reach the forecourt with the bike. Severe adds: no Notary gets
a photograph.

**Par and the runner's line.** 4:00. The side alleys at every checkpoint and
the grandstand jump, dismounting at the forecourt's east flank.

**Story in play.** Page in: "The ceremonial avenue, that night. The uprisings
are breaking enforcement from the edges in. The coalition needs a foothold at
the Forever Office before dawn. The Union built this road to march on. Ride."
Latch rides pillion on an ally's bike and never steers. Street screens loop
Voss with the official caption; some already show the pirate track, patched in
by someone in the crowd. The green wave is the most visible Inheritance help
before the wipe and stays deliberately ambiguous (**proposal**).

**Humor.** Parade signage: *Spontaneous celebration zone. Celebrate in single
file.* A checkpoint sign: *Freedom of movement is guaranteed within approved
lanes.*

**The moment.** The whole avenue's lights going green one after another ahead
of you, and Mara saying it wasn't us.
