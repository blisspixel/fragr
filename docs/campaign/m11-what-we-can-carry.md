# M11: What We Can Carry

**Status:** proposed level, unbuilt. Earth after the initial wipe, with restoration
still active. Target 20-25 minutes. [Treatment](../CAMPAIGN-MISSIONS.md#m11-what-we-can-carry).

## Story and people

Return to Low Water for survivors who could not get out. Latch refuses to abandon
captive agents and admits wanting to live too. Mara has a route to a refuge;
Tern's transport availability is finite. Renn can locate custody remnants but
cannot promise who remains. Prior rescues determine the people present and able
to help, not whether the mission has any valid completion path.

Edda's clinic lamp, Splice's tram and the meal table from M03 recur. Absence has
specific meaning because these places were inhabited before. A recovered backup
cannot hold the whole town. The mission lets people help one another without
calling survival a selection of the virtuous.

## A changed version of home

Enter from the former freight departure. The old route toward the market is
broken; use a visible service connection into the court. Preserve the skyline
and building footprints while changing usable topology and combat purpose.

| M03 landmark | New state | What the player does |
|---|---|---|
| Freight platform | Damaged and partly stripped | Establish return/exit staging and see why transport cannot reach everyone |
| Tram trench | Intermittent work zone with a readable boundary | Cross via cover or reopen a maintenance flank |
| Workshop | Ruined equipment with one intact service room | Recover people or memories; Splice can help if present |
| Market | Empty awnings and deliberate machine work | Move between rescue branches under changing local pressure |
| Clinic | Recognizable entrance, isolated safe interior | Reach survivors and secure their short evacuation path |
| Habitation court | Collapsed connection, intact alternate stair | Reach stranded residents without impossible jumps |
| Roof loop | Partly broken, new sightlines | Scout work zones and open a clear return shortcut |

No instantaneous lush regrowth. Immediate aftermath is damage, emptiness, residual
danger and people improvising. The years-later coda provides ecological recovery.

## Encounters and rescue design

Pavers control specific sectors, Collectors pressure movement, and a Surveyor
coordinates a crest where the player has several ways to break sight. Some
isolated Union forces still threaten people; others cooperate locally. Avoid
automatic faction-wide redemption or a restoration of the regime.

Required objective is a safe return route plus the main group. Optional rescues
concern reachable clinic and workshop groups. The final incompatible-choice
mechanism remains open; do not fabricate an unavoidable human-versus-agent choice.
If both can be reached through good play, let both survive.

Supplies are deliberate remains of the M03 stores and current evacuee equipment.
Secrets: an old rooftop stash whose clue was seen earlier, and a newly exposed
maintenance cabinet. Secret exploration cannot invisibly commit a casualty.

## States and continuation

Consume prior `medic_evacuated`, `technician_evacuated`, and recovered-mind state.
Proposed local flags identify each group found, route secured and group departed.
Departure confirmation shows known reachable people and what leaving means.
Preserve uncertainty about unobserved missing people rather than labeling them
dead merely because a Boolean was absent.

Checkpoints at return staging, after opening the service loop, and before final
withdrawal. Saves preserve rescue groups and route changes. Reopening a door
cannot respawn an already-departed person. Party wipe restores a checkpoint,
not a moral score or a new version of the entire world's history.

## Art, dialogue and humor

Use damaged variants of actual M03 assets and a changed soundscape: interrupted
machinery, distant work, fewer everyday voices. Let survivors make their own
small jokes if appropriate. No Host punchline over a death. Latch's disagreement
with a rushed departure is specific to someone the player can see and help.

## Acceptance

Run the full prior-rescue matrix, alternative paths, blocked NPC recovery,
departure/cancel, disconnect with a carried archive, checkpoint rollback and
four-player escape. Fresh players should recognize home, understand each rescue,
and have meaningful actions after the wipe. Do not call a tinted M03 map complete.
