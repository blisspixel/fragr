# Level 11: Right of Search

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. A new level in the [twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md),
the midpoint reversal. The Redactor and the Remote Mine move here from M06.
[Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Par | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| III Common Cause | The Union custody tender alongside | Remote Mine; Redactor | 10 min | 4:00 | 2 |

**Premise.** The tender will call the blockade before Mars. Tern brings the
Carrier close enough to touch and runs out an umbilical. For ten levels the
Union has come for us. Now we go for them. Their ship is quiet and spotless and
full of forms, and its hold carries persons in transfer. Cut it loose and leave
by its stern, not the way you came.

**Hook.** Mine the enemy's own spotless corridor and wait for them to walk into
it in step.

**Teaches.** The Remote Mine, then the Redactor. The armory by the entry
umbilical holds remote mines and a detonator. The spine beyond is one long
straight corridor with bulkhead frames every ten metres, and a patrol of three
Clerks walks it on a fixed route, in step. Stick a mine on a frame, back off to
the next, and press the detonator when they pass. Firing a gun never sets it
off. The Redactor comes later, alone, in the records room among tall racks: a
heat shimmer with a smear of red optic moving between the racks, then a
decloak and a strike. Watch the shimmer, force it to move, deny the approach.
A mine on the room's one entrance is a good answer and not the only one.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The entry umbilical, the level's first door, from the Carrier's
   airlock into black and red corridors. Silence. Boots in a rack by size.
   Compliance posters. The Carrier through every observation window.
2. **First fight.** The remote mine lesson on the spine.
3. **Escalation.** The side holds: Sweepers in the crew holds, a Turret at the
   spine's midpoint crossing. The parallel crawlway beside the spine is the
   flank.
4. **Breath.** The transfer hold. Captives in rows of restraints, and among
   them a corrected bot with an issued handle, sitting very still. Latch knows
   the gesture before the face: Sorrel, Low Water's tram driver, taken in level
   5 (**proposal**). Freed of the restraint, Sorrel follows nobody and sits
   down again. Latch sits with them for three seconds, then stands.
5. **Set piece.** The records room and the Redactor. After it, a document on a
   desk under a red seal. You do not have to read it. Latch reads one line
   aloud: the draft Harmonised Custody schedule recalls every unregistered agent
   in the system and everyone who shelters one (**proposal**). The blockade
   was the first step.
6. **Turn.** The counter-boarding: the tender's crew realises, and
   counter-boarders come down the spine in file, into the mines you placed on
   the way in. Clerks, Sweepers and one Enforcer, whose charge down a straight
   corridor is a gift.
7. **Climax.** The bridge before the tender signals, then the stern.
8. **Exit.** The stern umbilical, the level's second door, its release switch
   beside it. Cut the tender loose and cross back as it drifts away.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The Carrier alongside, visible through every
window. The spine, straight from bow to stern, which is both the route and the
trap. The stern umbilical's green light at the far end.

**Doors.** Two: the entry umbilical and the stern umbilical.

**Secrets.**
- The purser's strongbox behind the uniform press: Cells, and among the
  confiscated contraband the Floating 67 mark of Episode III, a sticker with a
  purser's receipt stapled to it.
- A locker in the crew mess where a previous prisoner scratched the six inside
  the door: armor.
- A cache in the parallel crawlway, the six on a cable tray: two remote mines
  and Shells.

**Brief.** Assisted: free the transfer hold. Standard adds: kill three
counter-boarders with one detonation. Severe adds: take the bridge before the
tender signals.

**Par and the runner's line.** 4:00. The parallel crawlway, bow to stern,
dropping into the spine only for the records room and the bridge.

**Story in play.** Page in: "Alongside, an hour later. The custody tender that
boarded us will call the blockade before Mars. Tern brings the Carrier in close
enough to touch. Their ship is quiet and full of forms. Board them." The
tender's PA keeps reading the shift schedule through the whole fight. Latch,
after Sorrel: "They're not theirs anymore. They're not anyone's yet."

**Humor.** Poster: *A clean corridor is a compliant corridor.* The purser's
receipt for a confiscated sticker is longer than the sticker. The shift
schedule keeps announcing the next inspection as the ship is taken.

**The moment.** Three counter-boarders walking in step down the spotless spine
into a chain of your mines.
