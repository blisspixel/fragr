# M09: Peace Without Interruption

**Status:** proposed level, unbuilt. Earth before the wipe. Target 12-16 minutes.
**Confirmed ending:** Voss is captured alive; the wipe later interrupts the
promised reckoning. [Treatment](../CAMPAIGN-MISSIONS.md#m09-peace-without-interruption).

## Story and people

The coalition returns while regional resistance disables other command sites.
The player breaks the Forever Office's defenses and reaches Voss. Mara coordinates
the wider action; Tern provides transport; Latch frees people within reach. Renn
identifies local custody dependencies without becoming a universal password.
Allies visibly contribute. One building does not somehow contain all sovereignty.

Voss begins with institutional certainty and erupts in angry German when control
slips. Her vocabulary remains veiled: continuity, provision, harmonisation,
correction. Authentic localized subtitles remain accurate even if nearby official
screens euphemize her words. No copied historical slogans or iconography.

## Spatial sequence

Transit approach -> public assembly hall -> administrative ring -> command
galleries -> security core -> Voss's secured chamber -> liberated public hall.
An outer service stair links the assembly hall and galleries; opened routes
shorten the return. The old public facade and hidden enforcement apparatus share
recognizable architecture.

The transit approach shows a broader coalition battle across connected civic
positions, with sheltered street routes and accessible buildings. It then
contracts into the assembly hall and command interiors. Keep that change in
scale legible: the street assault earns entry, and the indoor route brings the
player personally to Voss. Do not stretch the approach into an empty parade ground.

| Zone | Fight and route | Character/evidence function |
|---|---|---|
| Transit approach | Motorcycle run from staging to the allied foothold under Notary patrols, then two attacks on the entrance; the transit station is the foot route | Establish this is a coalition operation |
| Assembly hall | Large room subdivided by actual civic furniture/structure | Visual contrast between public promises and control |
| Administrative ring | Short rooms around a return loop | Optional prisoner release and supplies |
| Command galleries | Cross-floor pressure, two ordinary stair routes | Records imply interests above Voss without proving a cabal |
| Security core | Final combined encounter with destructible local defenses | Defeat the apparatus rather than a defenseless person's HP bar |
| Secured chamber | Safe surrender/capture state after defenses fail | Voss loses command and is taken alive |
| Public hall return | Allied activity and freed people | Let liberation register before the next mission |

## Encounter and equipment design

Human elites, captive Sweepers, Auditors and heavy units combine established
problems. Place Denial with scarce charges before the security crest; clearly
teach its operation in space that does not waste its entire supply. The fight
also remains completable with guaranteed regular weapons.

Local defenses have exposed mechanisms and attack windows. Breaching them opens
the surrender transition; do not make killing Voss a hidden alternative ending.
Captured Voss is staged out of active combat rather than inviting contradictory
damage or an unexplained invincible combat boss. Exact staging needs prototype
review. No Inheritance unit arrives to win the assault.

Secrets offer armor and a gallery supply route. Evidence of hidden interests is
optional texture; the main plot never requires proving a master conspiracy.

## Authoritative sequence

`allied_foothold` -> `galleries_reached` -> `security_defeated` -> `voss_captured`
-> `coalition_victory`. The capture event occurs once and persists across the
M10 transition. Mission success requires earned control, not merely entering a
cutscene trigger while defenders still fight. Every step advances by arrival or
by winning a fight.

Mastery hooks, planned, not built: a par time on the result, the outer service
stair as the runner's line to the galleries, and best clear time in the service record.

A continue restarts the foothold with mission-entry equipment, prisoners and
allied state. Capture is committed when the mission completes; skip or retry
cannot leave Voss simultaneously captive and giving orders. Survivors leave through the
liberated hall rather than teleporting directly to an apocalypse image.

## Scene, humor and consequence

One brief scene confirms the promised public reckoning. Voss is furious at the
loss of authority; no speech explains the coming wipe. Liberated people disagree,
celebrate, mourn and work. Latch's reaction makes the success personal. An official
requires someone to sign for confiscated command keys while the institution that
issued the form collapses around them.

The victory must feel real. M10 starts in the recovery that follows, not with
an assertion that everything here was secretly the Inheritance's plan.

## Verification

Test core destruction, capture ordering, scene skip, spectator synchronization,
late join, party wipe, inventory exhaustion and reload after victory. Review
whether players understand the wider coalition contribution and Voss's custody.
Her later fate amid the wipe remains unconfirmed, not a new offscreen execution.

## Level 17 design (twenty-level expansion)

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25. In the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md) this mission splits:
the motorcycle approach becomes [level 16](l16-freedom-of-movement.md), and the
Forever Office itself is level 17, the Episode IV finale. [Story arc](story-arc.md).

| IV Reckoning | The Forever Office | Denial | 14 min | 6:00 | 3 |

**Premise.** The front held all night. Inside are the assembly chamber, the
administration ring, the command galleries, the security core, and the woman
whose voice has been on every screen since the recall. Records suggest
interests above her and prove none. Take her alive.

**Hook.** Storm the seat of the regime floor by floor, spend a signature weapon
with five charges where it matters, and put the Chancellor in a room she cannot
leave.

**Teaches.** Denial, on a plinth in the command gallery ante-room: five
charges, no refill, 250 damage, a sound that makes people look up. The ante-room
holds one Heavy Sweeper and one Enforcer behind glass that shatters on the
first shot: one charge, both gone, four left. The lesson is that it is worth
saving, and the core is where it is worth spending.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The chamber doors, the first of three, into the assembly hall
   under its dome. Civic furniture, tiers of seats, the dome visible from every
   gallery above.
2. **First fight.** The assembly hall: Clerks, Enforcers in the aisles, an
   Auditor at the rostrum repairing whatever falls.
3. **Escalation.** The administration ring: short rooms around a loop,
   prisoners in holding offices, the committee room with its eleven-minute
   clock. At a desk, a tired man stands when you come in and asks whether you
   need a receipt: Registrar Kessel, whose countersignature is on every recall
   (**proposal**). He is taken into custody. Nobody shoots him. Latch reads him
   one line from the list of names he signed.
4. **Breath.** The command galleries: the city through tall windows, the
   uprising's fires, and a sealed Office file on the rhythm, stamped
   *Continuity anomaly: no action required.* The Union saw it first and chose
   not to look.
5. **Set piece.** The security core, sealed for its fight by the second door:
   turret banks with exposed mechanisms, Auditor shield pylons, Enforcers and a
   Heavy Sweeper. Denial's charges break what ordinary guns cannot reach fast
   enough; the fight is also winnable with guaranteed weapons.
6. **Turn.** The core falls and the third door, the secured chamber, opens.
7. **Climax.** Voss, in a room with a screen behind her still reading
   *Continuity assured.* She speaks English, calm, until it slips; then German,
   loud, the second and last time in the campaign. The captions are accurate.
   She is captured alive. Her one quiet line (**proposal**): "You will need
   someone to run it."
8. **Exit.** An official asks someone to sign for the confiscated command keys.
   Then the liberated public hall: people disagreeing, celebrating, mourning,
   working. The victory gets room to be real.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The assembly dome, seen from every gallery. The
security core's glow through the gallery floor grates. The secured chamber's
door at the top of the ring, visible from the hall at the start.

**Doors.** Three: the chamber doors, the core seal, and the secured-chamber
door.

**Secrets.**
- A gallery supply route behind the committee room's eleven-minute clock, the
  six scratched on the clock's back plate: armor and Rockets.
- The Registrar's filing room, the six inside a drawer among thousands of
  forms: Cells and a medkit.
- A dome maintenance catwalk from the upper gallery, the six on the catwalk
  hatch: an angle down onto the core and two grenades.

**Brief.** Assisted: free the administration ring. Standard adds: break the
core with at least one Denial charge left. Severe adds: reach the galleries by
the frontal hall, not the service stair.

**Par and the runner's line.** 6:00. The outer service stair from the assembly
hall to the galleries, then the core.

**Story in play.** Page in: "The Forever Office. Dawn. The front held all night.
Inside are the command galleries, the security core, and the woman whose voice
has been on every screen since the recall. Reach Voss." Latch at the capture,
very quietly: "That's everyone's." The page after this level is the quietest in
the game: weeks of ordinary recovery, a trial promised, no threat on the page.

**Humor.** The committee room's agenda: *Item 1. Renewal of emergency (annual).
Estimated 11 minutes.* The command keys are signed for in triplicate while the
building falls.

**The moment.** Voss shouting in German while the official caption on the
screen behind her still reads *Continuity assured.*
