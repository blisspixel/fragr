# Level 20: Local Exception

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. The last third of [M10](m10-all-systems-normal.md) in the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md): survival minutes 21
to 33, the climax. Surviving it unlocks [Still Here](epilogue-still-here.md).
[Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Clock | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V Inheritance | Waterworks, freight pier, refuge approach | Surveyor | 13 min | 12:00 | 1 |

**Premise.** Latch, Tern and the other free agents are somewhere in the
waterworks' control rooms, talking to the thing that absorbed every Union bot
on Earth, asking it for an exception for one refuge. You are on the pier where
four hundred people are waiting to cross, keeping them alive long enough for
the argument to matter. It is the need the whole campaign built: your friend's
choice, and you holding the line so they can make it.

**Hook.** Twelve minutes on a pier against machines that do not hate you,
while your friend argues for your life somewhere you cannot see.

**Teaches.** The Surveyor, the last new enemy: an elevated off-white unit on a
waterworks tower that projects a mark, a visible disc of light, onto a
position, and the nearby machines converge on the mark. The first Surveyor
works alone with one Collector below it, so the lesson is readable: break its
sight, move off the mark, or hit it during its exposed observation phase, when
its lens opens wide.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The waterworks: sluices, catwalks and a crew still running the
   pumps so the refuge has water. The clock picks up from level 19.
2. **First fight.** The Surveyor lesson over the sluice catwalks.
3. **Escalation.** The waterworks crew's crossing: absorbed Sweepers, a
   Collector at the valve house, a Paver striping the service road. Clear the
   crossing and the crew goes on its own.
4. **Breath.** The pier. Four hundred people on it and behind the refuge gate,
   Edda's lamp lit on a bollard if Edda was saved (someone else lit it if not),
   Splice and Orrin if they came, Sorrel sitting on a crate, not following
   anyone. Thirty seconds of the people you are here for.
5. **Set piece.** The pier holds. Three short approaches funnel onto it, from
   the waterworks, the freight yard and the tram trench, and they converge:
   Surveyors marking positions, Collectors working the pier's barricades,
   Pavers striping the freight yard, absorbed Heavy Sweepers and an Assessor
   over the water. Resupply is on the pier itself, in the crates people carried.
6. **Turn.** At the pier's screen, the harbour display, a single message: the
   exact number of people at this pier, that Latch has said each of their
   names, and that the work here closes in six minutes. It knows exactly whom
   you are saving and states the closure as if that knowledge changes nothing.
7. **Climax.** The last six minutes, the heaviest pressure of the campaign,
   readable to the end.
8. **Exit.** The reprieve. The machines reach the refuge line and stop, then
   flow around it like water around a stone and go on to the next district.
   Latch walks back across the pier. The refuge gate, the level's one door,
   opens.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The freight platform you left from in level 5,
now the refuge crossing. The waterworks tower where the free agents are, lit
from inside. Edda's lamp.

**Doors.** One: the refuge gate.

**Secrets.**
- Edda's lamp on the pier, lit; under its bollard, the six chalked by whoever
  lit it: armor.
- The waterworks valve house, the six on its door frame: Rockets.
- The pier crane's cab, reached by the jetpack, the six on the controls: Cells
  and a sightline over all three approaches.

**Brief.** Assisted: get the waterworks crew across. Standard adds: break a
Surveyor's mark before its machines arrive. Severe adds: finish with every
rescue from this episode alive.

**Par.** None. The result shows people helped and damage taken. Surviving the
clock, after surviving levels 18 and 19, unlocks the epilogue; exhausting the
continues here leads to the wipe-failure ending and credits.

**Story in play.** No page in; the clock carries straight on from level 19. The
message is the Inheritance's one precise contact in the wipe, text first,
voice optional, never a speech. Latch, back across the pier, quietly: "Held
them. We're even." The epilogue's final fragment lands later: "You went back for
them. I included that."

**Humor.** Tern, over the channel, once, near the end: "It's very polite. I hate
it." A callback to the Moon, and the only laugh in Episode V.

**The moment.** The machines stopping at the refuge line and walking around it.
