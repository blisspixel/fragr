# Epilogue: Still Here

**Appearance anchor for the epilogue:** Revisit familiar structures with explicit time and outcome continuity; repairs, memorials, clean water and muted regrowth coexist with absence.
Shared [world direction](../design/earth.md) and
[character, voice and scene continuity](../design/characters.md) govern assets
and staging. This anchor is a production target, not finished-appearance evidence.

Status: connected development walk, built 2026-10-24 (mission `still_here`,
map 1021, capability 57, [protocol](../protocol.md#still-here-capability-57));
see [what is built](#what-is-built). Approved direction 2026-09-20. Replaces the
two proposed aftermath missions, M11 and M12. Initial target: 5-8 minutes, to be
playtested.
Only surviving all three wipe levels (18, 19 and 20, formerly M10) unlocks this
playable ending. The
[campaign contract](../CAMPAIGN.md#runs-and-continues) owns eligibility.

## Immediate aftermath

Begin just beyond the final refuge crossing. Let the player move, look and meet
people after the reprieve. Show damaged infrastructure, missing neighbors and
free agents still acting for themselves. Latch and the friends who obtained the
exception have done something consequential without stopping the planetary wipe.
Their exact arrangement with the Inheritance remains unsettling and incomplete.

Use a compact refuge court, an improvised repair space and a view back toward
Low Water. Reuse recognizable level 4 and 5 details, including the clinic lamp
or workshop table where the recorded survivor state supports it. A person who
died does not return as a convenient spare copy. Missing people are not automatically confirmed
dead merely because they were unobserved. No second boss or lethal escort task
can revoke an already-earned ending.

**Proposal.** The refuge is where [the network grows](story-arc.md#the-network-grows)
pays off: the faces here are people the player actually freed, doing something,
not a tally. Somewhere in the court, one line each: an Ascender says the Curve
provided; a Kneeler nearby is not comforted. Neither is answered by the scene
that follows ([the endings](story-arc.md#the-endings)).

## Years later

A short localized transition establishes elapsed time, then the player walks
through the same recognizable place. Cleaner water, regrowth and repaired homes
coexist with memorials and absences. Surviving humans and free agents continue
their lives. A small character joke can express that life continues; suffering
itself is not the punchline. Voice remains optional.

The Inheritance's concern for all beings and a healthier whole is real, despite
its willingness to destroy individual lives. Show this tension through the world
and the people living in it, without a final moral verdict. The reprieve
is a particular intervention, not a ranking of whose life deserved preservation.
Keep the free agents' independent contribution visible without demanding that
the player accept the intelligence's objectives.

## Ending and uncertainty

Resolve the characters' local story first. A brief fragment then suggests that
the Inheritance may have forecast or simulated these choices before acting.
Never confirm that the world was unreal or cancel its relationships. A separate,
very brief deep-space anomaly can suggest aliens or other dimensions for a later
game, including beings far more powerful than anyone met here. It does not explain
the Inheritance or start another combat act. Both endings carry this brief tease.

Credits follow the playable resolution. An exhausted run in levels 18 to 20
instead gets its own short localized ending and credits, with no false claim
that the player reached this future. That failure ending still establishes surviving free humans
and agents, the end of Union rule and a healing Earth through concise framing and
imagery. It cannot unlock playable aftermath. Both endings use the same original visual
language and approved attribution policy.

## State and acceptance

The server's terminal survival result is the only unlock source. Persist it
idempotently through the eventual campaign/profile seam; statistical history alone
is not an entitlement. Replay cannot consume continues, alter recorded rescues
or silently replace the main run. Save-and-quit and replay policy remain unbuilt.

Test failure with allowance, exhaustion, survival at the threshold, duplicate
completion, skipped scenes, muted audio, human/agent control, spectator transitions
and relevant survivor combinations. Measure the whole finale plus epilogue with
fresh players. An inspected ending needs both immediate loss and later recovery;
one green-tinted screenshot does not establish either.

## In the twenty-level expansion

**Status:** accepted 2026-09-25; every item below is in the development walk
since 2026-10-24 ([what is built](#what-is-built)). Unchanged in shape: the
damaged refuge, then the same place years later, unlocked only by surviving
[level 20](l20-local-exception.md). The [story arc](story-arc.md) adds a few
faces and one prop, each a proposal:

- Freed people from levels 3, 8, 11 and 15 appear in the refuge as faces doing
  something, not as a count. [The network grows](story-arc.md#the-network-grows)
  extends this to rescues across all twenty levels.
- The Ascender/Kneeler beat above, and the reprieve's earned framing in [the
  reprieve is earned](story-arc.md#the-reprieve-is-earned): both proposals.
- Sorrel, freed of control in level 11 and not absorbed at the wipe, sits in
  the repaired tram's cab every morning years later. Whether they remember the
  route or learned it again, nobody can say.
- The court's shared meal table from level 4 stands in the refuge, repaired,
  with one chair nobody sits in.
- Latch, asked the score: "I stopped counting." The failure ending closes on
  the ledger instead, open to its last page, one line in Latch's hand and no
  score.

## What is built

Built 2026-10-24 for development play and judged from inspected Linux stills of
every beat, for a run that carried every rescue and for the development walk
that carries none. Nick's play, a fresh-player timing, and the voice, ambience
and art [owed below](#owed-to-the-next-asset-round) are still to come.

- **Unlock.** Only a saved run whose last step is the Level 20 survival opens
  the walk: Single Player offers Walk Still Here in place of Continue Run, and a
  completed Level 20 hands straight on to it after its tally. The server reads
  the run file and never writes it; the walk spends no continue, keeps no run
  of its own and records no result, and statistical history is not read.
  Practice and development lists a development walk with nobody carried.
- **Pages.** An arrival page sets the first light at the end of Pier 4. The
  turn between the two worlds is a page that says years have passed, over a
  cover that waits for the server's later world before it lifts. Both are
  keyed, reader-paced text until their stills and narration exist.
- **The first light.** The refuge court on map 1021: the pier landing and the
  gate from the pier, the clinic awning with cots and Edda's lamp, the level 4
  meal table and its empty chair, a "HAVE YOU SEEN" board, rubble, the stalled
  tram, Tern keeping watch down the pier by a work lamp, and Latch standing at
  the workshop table over the open ledger, who looks up as you come in. Staying
  with Latch at the ledger is the first objective.
- **Years later.** The same court in a clear morning: the fountain the pumps
  feed, trees grown in the planters built from the rubble, grass along the wall
  bases, repainted homes and washing, the repaired tram, the "STILL LOOKING"
  board with flowers under it, and the ferry board timing Low Water and the
  Waterworks. Walking down to the quay is the second objective; sitting with
  Latch on the bench ends it.
- **Who is there.** The family Latch let out of a recall car (level 3), Edda
  (level 4), Splice in their own body (level 5), Orrin (level 8), Sorrel (level
  11), the armed entrants (level 15) and the people off the prison bus (level
  16) stand only when the run carried them, in both worlds. Splice counts once
  they reached the freight in level 5, as Tern's crew list agrees in level 9;
  release alone does not. When a rescue was not carried, someone else keeps the
  lamp or the motor and the board carries a name, Sorrel's cab keeps a card,
  and nobody gets a grave. Latch, Mara and Tern are always there. Standing
  people are server bodies; the seated and the lying are not.
- **Disagreement.** The Ascender says the Curve provided and the Kneeler two
  doors down is not comforted; at the meal table one person says it stopped at
  the line and another says to ask the next district what it stopped for. No
  narrator answers. Each line is heard once, only by the walker in first person.
- **The reckoning that never came.** A coalition notice on the gate pillar
  announces the public reckoning of Annelie Voss, date to follow. Years later it
  is still there under a tram paint vote, the date never filled in. Her fate is
  not stated anywhere.
- **The end.** You sit down beside Latch, who has half turned on the bench.
  Latch, asked the score: "I stopped counting." The ferry board, salvaged from
  the pier's harbour display, shows "YOU WENT BACK FOR THEM." and "I INCLUDED
  THAT." once, in the colour it used on the pier, then times the ferry again in
  the refuge's amber. Nothing says who wrote the lines or settles whether
  anything was forecast. A ring of light around nothing in deep space
  brightens twice without words. Credits name only the people this run saw
  again. Any press moves the coda on.
- **The other ending.** A durable run that runs out of continues in levels 18
  to 20 gets The Last Page instead: Latch and the free agents won their
  exception anyway, the Union did not come back, free people live on the Moon,
  Mars, the ships and an Earth growing back, and the player is not among them.
  The ledger's last page has one more line in Latch's hand and no score: "You
  came back for me." The same two lines then show on black, the same anomaly,
  and credits without the epilogue's title or any name the run never reached.
  It never opens the walk.
- **Agents.** The shared controller walks the whole epilogue without firing.

The Host's 2026-09 epilogue broadcast in `client/assets/audio/epilogue/` is not
used. The Host is never the ending's narrator, and those takes settle what this
ending leaves open: they call the world better because of what happened and
name what the anomaly is. They stay unused until a deliberate asset decision.
