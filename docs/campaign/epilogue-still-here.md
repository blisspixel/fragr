# Epilogue: Still Here

Status: approved direction, unbuilt, 2026-09-20. Replaces the two proposed
aftermath missions, M11 and M12. Initial target: 5-8 minutes, to be playtested.
Only surviving all three wipe levels (18, 19 and 20, formerly M10) unlocks this
playable ending. The
[campaign contract](../CAMPAIGN.md#runs-and-continues) owns eligibility.

## Immediate aftermath

Begin just beyond the final refuge crossing. Let the player move, look and meet
people after the reprieve. Show damaged infrastructure, missing neighbors and
free agents still acting for themselves. Latch and the friends who obtained the
exception have done something consequential without stopping the planetary wipe.
Their exact arrangement with the Inheritance remains unsettling and incomplete.

Use a compact refuge court, an improvised repair space and a view back toward
Low Water. Reuse recognizable M03 details, including the clinic lamp or workshop
table where the recorded survivor state supports it. A person who died does not
return as a convenient spare copy. Missing people are not automatically confirmed
dead merely because they were unobserved. No second boss or lethal escort task
can revoke an already-earned ending.

**Proposal.** The refuge is where [the network grows](story-arc.md#the-network-grows)
pays off: the faces here are people the player actually freed, doing something,
not a tally. Somewhere in the court, one line each: an Ascender says the Curve
provided; a Kneeler nearby is not comforted. Neither is answered by the scene
that follows ([the endings](story-arc.md#the-endings)).

## Years later

A short localized transition establishes elapsed time, then the player walks
through the same recognizable place. Cleaner water, regrowth and repaired homes
coexist with memorials and absences. Surviving humans and free agents continue
their lives. A small character joke can express that life continues; suffering
itself is not the punchline. Voice remains optional.

The Inheritance's concern for all beings and a healthier whole is real, despite
its willingness to destroy individual lives. Show this tension through the world
and the people living in it, without a final moral verdict. The reprieve
is a particular intervention, not a ranking of whose life deserved preservation.
Keep the free agents' independent contribution visible without demanding that
the player accept the intelligence's objectives.

## Ending and uncertainty

Resolve the characters' local story first. A brief fragment then suggests that
the Inheritance may have forecast or simulated these choices before acting.
Never confirm that the world was unreal or cancel its relationships. A separate,
very brief deep-space anomaly can suggest aliens or other dimensions for a later
game, including beings far more powerful than anyone met here. It does not explain
the Inheritance or start another combat act. Both endings carry this brief tease.

Credits follow the playable resolution. An exhausted M10 failure instead gets
its own short localized ending and credits, with no false claim that the player
reached this future. That failure ending still establishes surviving free humans
and agents, the end of Union rule and a healing Earth through concise framing and
imagery. It cannot unlock playable aftermath. Both endings use the same original visual
language and approved attribution policy.

## State and acceptance

The server's terminal survival result is the only unlock source. Persist it
idempotently through the eventual campaign/profile seam; statistical history alone
is not an entitlement. Replay cannot consume continues, alter recorded rescues
or silently replace the main run. Save-and-quit and replay policy remain unbuilt.

Test failure with allowance, exhaustion, survival at the threshold, duplicate
completion, skipped scenes, muted audio, human/agent control, spectator transitions
and relevant survivor combinations. Measure the whole finale plus epilogue with
fresh players. An inspected ending needs both immediate loss and later recovery;
one green-tinted screenshot does not establish either.

## In the twenty-level expansion

**Status:** planned, accepted 2026-09-25. Unchanged in shape: the damaged refuge, then
the same place years later, unlocked only by surviving
[level 20](l20-local-exception.md). The [story arc](story-arc.md) adds a few
faces and one prop, each a proposal:

- Freed people from levels 3, 8, 11 and 15 appear in the refuge as faces doing
  something, not as a count. [The network grows](story-arc.md#the-network-grows)
  extends this to rescues across all twenty levels.
- The Ascender/Kneeler beat above, and the reprieve's earned framing in [the
  reprieve is earned](story-arc.md#the-reprieve-is-earned): both proposals.
- Sorrel, freed of control in level 11 and not absorbed at the wipe, sits in
  the repaired tram's cab every morning years later. Whether they remember the
  route or learned it again, nobody can say.
- The court's shared meal table from level 4 stands in the refuge, repaired,
  with one chair nobody sits in.
- Latch, asked the score: "I stopped counting." The failure ending closes on
  the ledger instead, open to its last page, one line in Latch's hand and no
  score.
