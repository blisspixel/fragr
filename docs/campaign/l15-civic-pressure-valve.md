# Level 15: Civic Pressure Valve

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. A new level in the [twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md),
the one new environment kit. The Article Blade moves here from M08.
[Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Par | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IV Reckoning | The sanctioned games stadium, the capital | Article Blade | 12 min | 5:00 | 2 |

**Premise.** The Union's sanctioned games are its supply line: entrants who
lose badly enough, or owe enough, come out the other side as corrections with
issued handles ([the games](../lore/the-chancellery.md#the-games)). Tonight's
games run as scheduled, in the stadium where Voss gave the opening address.
The entrants hold numbered placards and nothing else. The uprising starts
here. You bring the guns.

**Hook.** Break the Union's bloodsport from the inside, with a sword off its
own plinth, while a pirate announcer calls it like a title fight.

**Teaches.** The Article Blade, on its plinth at the centre of the floor, as in
every venue: seventy damage a swing, twelve swings, then it goes back to its
plinth. The first wave of the bout comes up the floor lifts in a tight ring
around the plinth, three Sweepers close enough that the Blade is plainly the
best tool in the room. Twelve swings is not many. The lesson is spending them.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The entrants' pen under the stands. Numbered placards on
   hooks, entrants who have not slept, a Clerk post at the pen gate. Latch
   opens the crate you brought and starts handing out Pistols without a
   speech.
2. **First fight.** The pen gate: three Clerks, then the players' tunnel, the
   first of two doors, onto the floor.
3. **Escalation.** The bout. The Union closes the arena gates by its own rules;
   they are the level's second door and seal only for this fight. The Blade
   lesson on the first wave.
4. **Set piece.** Waves of wardens: Sweepers up the floor lifts, Clerks and an
   Enforcer from the tunnels, Notaries overhead doubling as broadcast cameras,
   their flash a camera flash going out live. The floor, the stands and the
   tunnels loop; armed entrants fight beside you and can fall.
5. **Breath.** Between waves the stadium announcer, official and chipper, reads
   the standings. The podium where Voss spoke is still dressed for ceremony.
   Her recorded address plays on the big screen, English only.
6. **Turn.** The broadcast booth on the upper ring, on the route: two Clerks
   and an Auditor. Take it and the screen switches to the pirate subtitle
   track. Same audio, honest words. The Host cuts in on the stadium feed and
   calls the rest of the bout like a league night.
7. **Climax.** The last wave, the heaviest: a Heavy Sweeper, an Assessor over
   the floor, Enforcers from both tunnels, while the whole stadium watches the
   honest captions. The gates open when it is won.
8. **Exit.** The ceremonial gate onto the avenue, into level 16.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The podium and the screen above it, captioning
in the official track, then the pirate one. The Blade's plinth at the centre.
The broadcast booth's lit window on the upper ring, visible from the floor.

**Doors.** Two: the players' tunnel, and the arena gates as one seal for the
bout.

**Secrets.**
- A confiscated weapons bin under the podium, the six scratched on its lid by
  somebody who knew: Rockets and three grenades.
- The Floating 67 mark of Episode IV: placard 67, hanging alone on its own
  hook in the pen, never issued.
- The concourse ring behind the stands, the six on a turnstile: armor.

**Brief.** Assisted: arm the entrants' pen. Standard adds: win one gate wave
using only the Article Blade. Severe adds: finish the bout with no armed
entrant fallen.

**Par and the runner's line.** 5:00. Straight from the tunnel to the plinth,
booth by the stand stair on the east side, never leaving the loop.

**Story in play.** Page in: "Earth. The capital, nine days after Mars. The
coalition is home, and the uprising needs a first spark. The sanctioned games
run tonight, as scheduled. The entrants hold numbered placards and nothing
else. Arm them." The Host takes the call after the booth, in his league
register, capitals and adrenaline: "LOW WATER IS DOMINATING THE FLOOR." "THE
WARDENS FADE INTO LAST." It is the level's Rock n' Roll Racing moment and the
only time the Host matters on screen. With radio muted, the pirate captions
carry the same turn.

**Humor.** The official announcer: "Entrant four-four-seven-one is OUT OF
COMPLIANCE!" Losing your placard is a separate offence from losing the bout.
Fatalities are recorded as unscheduled retirements, on a form the deceased
must countersign. The countersignature on the correction pathway form is the
same one from Latch's recall notice (**proposal**).

**The moment.** The Host cutting in on the stadium feed and calling the
uprising like a title fight while the screen runs the honest captions.
