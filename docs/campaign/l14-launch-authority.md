# Level 14: Launch Authority

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. The second half of [M08](m08-weight-of-permission.md) in the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md): the exterior launch
works, the jeep and the Continuance Walker. The Episode III finale and the
campaign's one combined-arms battle. [Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Par | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| III Common Cause | The Martian launch works | Jeep; Continuance Walker | 14 min | 6:00 | 0 |

**Premise.** Allies hit four other sites across the plain at the same time.
This one is ours. The ships on the pads are the coalition's way back to Earth,
and the works' authority is a Continuance Walker, a machine the Union built to
guard what it cannot staff.

**Hook.** Jump a berm in a captured jeep while a walking artillery platform
shells the ground behind you, then finish it on foot under the gantry.

**Teaches.** The jeep, then the Walker. The depot defense is fought on foot
first. Under a Union tarp in the depot yard sits the jeep: walk up, press Use,
drive. The first use of its mounted heat gun is a Notary pair over the depot
road, from the gunner seat with the jeep parked, the way it was always done in
1942. The Walker comes alone for its first phase, across a broad bounded field:
a stomp ring marked on the ground before it lands, an artillery lane painted by
a line of red markers, then a relocation and an exposed recovery with its
joint vents open. Supporting units only arrive in later phases, from visible
lanes.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The freight lift opens onto daylight: red rock, the launch
   gantry on the horizon, the depot yard below.
2. **First fight.** The depot defense on foot: Clerks behind sandbags, a
   Turret on the depot roof, Sweepers from the vehicle sheds.
3. **Escalation.** The jeep, then the bermed approach: bunkers with Clerks,
   Ranged Sweepers on the berm crests, a Turret at the bend. The jeep circuit
   runs around the outside; infantry trenches cut through the middle. Either
   works.
4. **Breath.** A berm-top overlook where the flares of the other sites go up
   across the plain, one after another. Mara on the channel: "Site two is
   ours. Site four is ours." Short, and then the ground shakes.
5. **Set piece.** The Continuance Walker. Phase one alone. Phase two brings a
   Sweeper squad from the pad road and a Notary pair. Phase three brings an
   Enforcer squad from the gantry and the Walker relocating to the pad apron.
   Resupply sits in trench bunkers on both flanks.
6. **Turn.** The Walker falls across the pad apron. The pad lights change from
   red to white.
7. **Climax.** The gantry base: the Union's last squad holding the control
   room at the foot of the gantry.
8. **Exit.** Reach the gantry control. The ships are ours.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The launch gantry, visible from the lift, the
target of every route. The Walker itself, taller than everything but the
gantry. The other sites' flares on the horizon.

**Doors.** None. The circuit is open ground; nothing has to be unlocked.

**Secrets.**
- Gantry ledge armor, reached by a readable jump from a bunker roof, the six
  on the ledge's railing.
- A trench bunker with the six chalked over its firing slit: Rockets and
  Cells.
- The motor pool's repair bay, the six on a toolbox: medkits and armor, the
  place to limp to after losing the jeep.

**Brief.** Assisted: break the depot defense. Standard adds: disable the
Walker without losing the jeep. Severe adds: disable the Walker on foot.

**Par and the runner's line.** 6:00. The jeep circuit with the berm jump
shortcut, dismounting at the Walker's first recovery.

**Story in play.** Page in: "The launch works, the same afternoon. Allies are
hitting four other sites at once; this one is ours. The ships on the pads are
the way home, and the Walker is the works' authority. Break the Walker." Tern,
arriving on the pad after the fight: "Liberation. Lovely. Who's paying for the
pad?" The page out closes Episode III: the coalition fleet lifts for Earth.

**Humor.** The jeep's dashboard placard: *Vehicle speed is a privilege.* Tern
presents the coalition with a repair invoice for the pad before the smoke
clears.

**The moment.** Jumping the berm in the jeep as the Walker's artillery walks up
the ground behind you.
