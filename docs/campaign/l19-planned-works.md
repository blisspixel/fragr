# Level 19: Planned Works

**Status:** proposed, 2026-09-25, unbuilt, pending Nick's choice of campaign
length. The middle third of [M10](m10-all-systems-normal.md) in the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md): survival minutes 10
to 21, the concourse and changed Low Water, the dark night.
[Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Clock | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V Inheritance | Evacuation concourse, then changed Low Water | Jetpack; Paver | 12 min | 11:00 | 0 |

**Premise.** The only crossing to the waterfront refuge runs through the
evacuation concourse and then through Low Water. Home, again. The roofs you
escaped across in level 5 are marked out in white work strips; the machines
are going to take the district apart and give the ground back to the river.
Former Union personnel help carry people for a few minutes and are not
absolved for it. And on the water tank where you and Latch used to argue about
tram motors, Latch decides to leave.

**Hook.** Fly over the roofs of your own home with a jetpack while the
machines draw demolition lines on the streets below.

**Teaches.** The jetpack, then the Paver. The jetpack is coalition gear in the
concourse stores. The first use is the concourse's broken stair to the
mezzanine: hold Jump in the air, feel the thrust, land on the gallery. Three
seconds of fuel, a visible flame, a gauge. The Paver comes alone on Low
Water's tram street: a broad matte body that projects a bounded white strip
onto the ground, prepares for a readable span, then works everything inside
the strip. Step outside it, or hit the Paver during preparation and the strip
goes out.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The evacuation concourse, two levels, full of people moving.
   A Clerk in a black coat helping carry a stretcher asks, from habit, to see a
   form. Latch: "Nobody's filing anymore."
2. **First fight.** The concourse crossing: absorbed Sweepers and Notaries at
   the far end, a Collector working the turnstiles. The jetpack lesson on the
   broken stair.
3. **Escalation.** Low Water's edge: the Paver lesson on the tram street, then
   the market from level 4 with two Pavers striping it and absorbed Heavy
   Sweepers walking the lines.
4. **Breath.** The court's shared meal table from level 4, still set, under a
   white strip. Then the water tanks. Latch sits on the tank and says the only
   despairing line they have in the game: "Every one I didn't reach." Tern, on
   the channel: the free agents are going to talk to it. Latch stands up: "I'm
   going. Hold them for me. Then we're even." There is no button to stop
   them. They go.
5. **Set piece.** The roof loop from level 5 run in reverse with the jetpack,
   over the strips, absorbed Notaries at roof height, a Paver on the workshop
   roof. The ground route through the streets always works too.
6. **Turn.** From the roofs you see the waterworks and the pier beyond: level
   5's freight platform, now the refuge crossing, lit.
7. **Climax.** The tram trench, marked end to end, a Paver at each end and a
   Collector working Splice's workshop door. Get through before the strips
   close.
8. **Exit.** The trench mouth to the waterworks, into level 20.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The same water tanks from level 5, now
surrounded by white strips. Edda's clinic sign, dark. The pier's lights, which
are where you are going.

**Doors.** None.

**Secrets.**
- Splice's workshop stash, found only if you remember where the six was on the
  tram service bay in level 5: Rockets and armor.
- The Floating 67 mark of Episode V: the tram paint vote poster in the market,
  *Green: 67*, with the mark tucked behind it.
- The market board, the six on its frame: medkits.

**Brief.** Assisted: get the concourse crossing clear. Standard adds: disable a
Paver during its preparation. Severe adds: take no work-strip damage.

**Par.** None. The clock is fixed; the result shows people helped and damage
taken.

**Story in play.** Page in, two lines, the clock paused while it shows: "The
evacuation concourse, minutes later. The only way to the pier runs through Low
Water." The rest of the story is in the place: the meal table, the dark clinic
sign, the tanks. Latch's departure is not a cutscene; it happens beside you on
the tank, in control, in three lines, and then Latch drops off the tank and runs
for the waterworks alone.

**Humor.** Almost none. The Clerk asking for a form is the only joke, and it is
not really one.

**The moment.** Latch on the water tank, choosing to go, and the white work
strips across every street you grew up on.
