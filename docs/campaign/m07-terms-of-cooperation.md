# M07: Terms of Cooperation

**Status:** proposed, unbuilt. Mars before the wipe. Target 10-14 minutes.
[Treatment](../CAMPAIGN-MISSIONS.md#level-12-terms-of-cooperation).

## Story and character work

Independent Martian communities agree the Union is a threat but keep bargaining
over responsibilities. Mara cannot turn sympathy into timely action. A habitat
is attacked before help arrives. The player intervenes and sees the cost of that
delay. Local leaders eventually commit real resources; do not reveal them all as
secret villains to simplify the political problem.

Latch recognizes captive workers among the force and residents. Renn supplies
useful knowledge while encountering justified mistrust. Edda can treat people
if saved; Splice can point out a safer route. Without them, the player simply
fights through the utility galleries. Orrin, if restored, has a small coherent
relationship beat rather than becoming a magic technical solution.

## Habitat layout

| Zone | Ordinary purpose and connections | Encounter role |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure arrival hall | Transit into a working settlement, visible inhabited branches | Brief context, supplies and an understandable emergency |
| Market street | Low buildings and enclosed cross-passages | Human/Sweeper patrols; two tactical approaches |
| Greenhouse trench | Food production with crossing bridges and service recesses | First Arc lesson against the armored Assessor drone, with safe flank |
| Pumping court | Water/air infrastructure, not a decorative courtyard | Main defensive crest, meaningful cover and retreat |
| Residential shelter | Safe interior reached after adjacent threats are cleared | People move on their own once the court is clear; no escort |
| Utility galleries | Loop behind the court and market | Alternate route, optional supplies and worker rescue |
| Mutual-aid depot | Actual incoming help and equipment | Resolution and concrete commitment to M08 |

Mars reads through protected habitation, rock, dust and greenhouse color. Do not
fill the level with vast empty red terrain. Exterior links are brief and bounded.
The player sees the resources everyone was negotiating over in ordinary use.

## Encounter and supply arc

Arrival disruption -> market skirmish -> greenhouse flank -> pumping-court crest
-> clear the shelter approach -> rendezvous with the help that finally arrives.
Mix human security, captive Sweepers and one or two established elite roles.
Introduce Arc with guaranteed Cells and a visible armor counter. No new enemy
subsystem is needed merely because the planet changed; the Assessor is a heavier
variant of the M03 drone ([ENEMIES](../ENEMIES.md#union-drones)).

Secrets: a greenhouse service shelf with armor, a supply cache behind a damaged
maintenance hatch, and an overlook into the court. Players without secret guns
or earlier rescue bonuses can complete the entire mission.

## State and consequences

Required state: `shelter_route_secured` (the pumping court is clear),
`aid_force_arrived` (reach the depot), then `coalition_commitment`. Objective
lines: "Hold the court", "Reach the depot". The player's action earns the local
rescue; broader losses caused by delay remain authored facts, not falsely
reported as an optional-objective failure.

A continue restarts at habitat arrival with prior outcomes intact. Help by
surviving characters changes tasks or staging; a retry cannot duplicate them.
Avoid a commander agreeing simply because an arbitrary kill counter fills.
Make the change visible in arriving vehicles, people and unlocked supplies.

## Evidence and tone

Shared infrastructure records show procurement and optimization demands from
several sides. They establish distributed dependence, not the complete origin
of the Inheritance. Essential meaning comes in short exchanges outside combat.

Humor targets coalition self-importance and endless amendments: a shipment can
have six competing declarations of independence attached to the same crate.
The punchline is not that independence was inherently foolish or victims deserved
to be abandoned. People act to improve what failed.

## Allies and validation

Mastery hooks, planned, not built: a par time on the result, the utility galleries
as the runner's line behind the court, and best clear time in the service record.

Test every previous rescue state, arrival trigger and mission retry. Inspect the habitat at walking height,
including safe spaces and background life. Players should understand both why
Mars matters and why help arrived late.

## Level 12 design (twenty-level expansion)

**Status:** planned, accepted 2026-09-25. Level 12 of the
[twenty-level expansion](../plans/campaign-expansion.md), the same mission.
[Story arc](story-arc.md).

| Episode | Place | New | First run | Par | Doors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| III Common Cause | Martian pressure habitat | Arc; Assessor | 12 min | 5:00 | 1 |

**Premise.** Mars believed the evidence and kept negotiating. The Union reached
one habitat, Cinder Row (**proposal**, working name), before the help did. You
arrive to help the people everyone meant to help. Nobody here is a secret
villain; they were just slower than a form.

**Hook.** Arrive at a siege that should never have happened, and pull a heavy
drone out of the sky onto its own squad.

**Teaches.** The Arc, then the Assessor. The Arc lies in a habitat maintenance
bay before the greenhouse trench, fed by Cells. The bay holds one Heavy
Sweeper behind a half-shut blast shield: its broad front armor soaks Rifle
fire, and the Arc goes straight through it. The Assessor comes alone over the
greenhouse trench: twice a Notary's span, armored belly, a launcher that
unfolds while two red optics count down with a rising chirp, three slow
visible canisters, then glowing rear vents through a long recovery. Its plates
halve bullets; the Arc and splash ignore them. Kill it over a squad and its
wreck lands on them.

**Shape.**
1. **Arrival.** The pressure arrival hall. The first greenhouse on the right is
   broken; frost on the plants, the lights still on. People shelter in the
   hall and look at you as if you are late, because you are.
2. **First fight.** Market street: human and Sweeper patrols, two approaches,
   enclosed cross-passages.
3. **Escalation.** The Arc lesson in the maintenance bay.
4. **Set piece.** The greenhouse trench: bridges over rows of crops, service
   recesses, the Assessor over it all and a Clerk squad pushing down the
   trench floor under its canisters.
5. **Breath.** The shelter approach: families behind a pressure door, the
   level's one door, and a crate in the lobby with six competing declarations
   of independence taped to it.
6. **Climax.** The pumping court: the crest held around air and water
   machinery. Sweepers and Clerks on two lanes, an Enforcer from the utility
   gallery, a Notary pair overhead. Stray fire can damage the pumps, visibly.
7. **Turn.** Real trucks at the mutual-aid depot. The habitats voted, and this
   time the vote came with drivers.
8. **Exit.** Reach the depot.

**Landmarks and sightlines.** The greenhouse glass lit green over red rock, seen
from every street. The pumping court's towers. The depot's floodlights at the
far end, which come on when the trucks arrive.

**Doors.** One: the shelter door, opened from beside it once the court is clear.

**Secrets.**
- Armor on the greenhouse service shelf, the six drawn in the condensation on
  the glass above it.
- An early Rocket Launcher with four Rockets behind a damaged maintenance
  hatch, the six painted on the hatch in red rock dust.
- An overlook into the court from the utility galleries, the six on its grate:
  Cells and a medkit.

**Brief.** Assisted: clear the shelter approach. Standard adds: drop the
Assessor on its own squad. Severe adds: hold the court with the pumps
undamaged.

**Par and the runner's line.** 5:00. The utility galleries behind the court,
entering the crest from its flank.

**Story in play.** Page in: "Mars, eleven days out. The habitats believed the
evidence and kept negotiating. The Union reached Cinder Row first. Help is
still a vote away. Hold the pumping court." Mara on the channel, not arguing
for once: "They're coming. I'm sorry it took this." A route panel shows
optimization demands from several sides, commercial and civic; nobody has to
read it.

**Humor.** Six declarations of independence on one crate, each disputing the
others' authority to declare. A resident: "We agreed on the trucks. We're
still arguing about the crate."

**The moment.** Bringing the Assessor down onto its own squad in the greenhouse
trench.
