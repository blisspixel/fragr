The ordinary routes start at the authored lift. They do not move the participant
directly, grant equipment, rewrite HP or skip combat phases. They collect the
arrival armor and depot Rocket/bullet supplies by walking, seek nearby health
when hurt, and use the shared finite equipment decisions. The human policy
reloads its current empty magazine and moves laterally during the final gantry
fight. Removing or destroying the jeep is the explicit setup variation, not
an action offered to a player. These fast, accurate runs are authoring checks,
not measured human completion times.

Controller failures exposed two substantive integration issues. Combat-goal
navigation preserves stationary firing when its goal is visible; using a
staging point there could stop a fighter whose actual joint target was hidden.
The controller now walks explicitly until a registered joint is visible and
within the selected weapon's range. Artillery avoidance also continues through
actual shell flight, chooses a supported perpendicular destination and keeps
visible support targets engaged while moving. Routing and the shared body
avoidance both receive actual translated Walker and vehicle collision.

`client/golden/m14_fact_vectors.json` is emitted by a deterministic native test
and compared as exact serialized bytes. It includes the actual intact/fallen
MapInfo, full snapshots, briefing, the solo attack cycle, disabled shells still
in flight, fallen support, final control, departure and retry. Arranged guard
clearance uses the canonical damage/death lifecycle, retaining valid dead
identities and dropped equipment. Invalid mission facts cover early disable,
invented fall/support, the wrong original jeep identity and unknown fields.
Independent review also found that first-cycle completion and on-foot loss
needed to require actual approach clearance. The native and client boundaries
now enforce that condition. Three additional invalid vectors use a real
in-progress arrival state; early jeep loss remains valid. The focused native
golden test passed again in `.agents/m14-fact-guard-final.log`.
The fixture is boundary evidence; arranged progression is not ordinary-route
evidence.

No enemy pressure, Walker timing, campaign rules revision or damage constants
were reduced to make these routes pass. No paid service was used. Actual
driving around the full circuit, rendered fight/transition inspection, the
three difficulty judgments and fresh-player readability remain separate gates.
