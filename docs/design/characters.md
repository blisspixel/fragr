# Character, voice and scene continuity

Production contract, 2026-09-30. [Cast](../lore/cast.md) owns roles and the
proposed visual anchors; [voice](../lore/voice.md) owns registers and recorded
wording. This guide keeps representations in agreement. It does not establish
unapproved faces, accents, ages, final reference art or cloned voices.

Use stable actor/role IDs in reference records. Distinguish proposed, reviewed,
implemented and recorded details. A generation or temporary narration voice
does not approve a new character identity.

| Reference field | Record once and reuse |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable role ID, working/public name, body type, canon/proposal status |
| Shape | Doorway-scale reference, proportions, front/side/back views, key silhouette features |
| Surface | Named palette roles, material families, outfit, equipment and deliberate personal repairs |
| Motion | Familiar stance, gestures, weapon handling, travel gait and speaking behavior |
| Voice | Register, delivery/rhythm, pronunciation, approved voice/model ID and casting status; permitted reference basis |
| Evidence | Source/reference hashes, assets, existing clips, reviewed still and played/motion receipt |
| Change | Mission, story reason and outcome condition for damage, repair, gear, restraint, rank or body changes |

Latch remains a free embodied person across worlds. Released restraints are
imposed equipment; movement and repaired chassis distinguish the individual.
Mara remains a logistics organizer rather than becoming a generic officer.
Renn's old issued kit and deliberately removed rank marking carry defection.
Tern's chassis, harness and practiced ship handling persist. Edda and Splice
remain identifiable through practical equipment and gestures. Orrin's changed
body belongs to an explicit restoration event. The player retains their chosen
body and callsign. The Inheritance has no default humanoid spokesperson.

Read the cast's proposed anchors for exact reference text. Do not infer ally,
hostility, consciousness or survival from chassis family, callsign or optics.
The server's actor identity and retained campaign outcomes decide participation;
art communicates those facts. Missing survivor branches need alternative mission
completion, not a cinematic that silently revives someone.

## Voice continuity

Keep a small original cast with distinct intent and conversational rhythm.
Record pronunciation and register alongside the voice selection. Match actual
captions to spoken wording, and track regeneration when text changes. Localized
captions remain separate from images and clips. Model/provider migration records
preserve the prior take and disclose the changed production contract.

Neutral scene narration is a separate role from Latch, Mara, Edda and the Host.
The [current transition batch](../plans/campaign-transition-audio.md) uses a stock
neutral narrator for existing framing text; it establishes no named character's
voice. The Host retains the lore's match and off-match registers and does not
become the campaign dispatcher. Paid voice work is offline development, under
the shared cap, with request receipts; no player API request is introduced.

## Stills first, consistent film later

Use the [existing scene plan](../plans/campaign-scenes.md) and
[film plan](../plans/cutscene-film.md). A reusable scene record holds its frozen
caption/script, shot list, world/time, actor references, explicit outcomes,
palette, light, asset paths, narration, skip behavior and evidence. The same
record governs gameplay-adjacent scenes, narrated stills and eventual video.

Approve identity references before producing a sequence. Keep the same
proportions, clothing, repaired parts, scale, lights and palette across cuts.
Use body-neutral framing where a fixed clip cannot represent the player's
chosen body. Do not bake essential words into pixels. A new shot cannot create
a rescue, inventory reward, objective or survival outcome. Captions and text
fallback remain complete when voice, pictures or video are unavailable.

Film stays behind its playable-campaign quality gate. A polished slideshow can
reuse final scripts and references today without paying for drifting character
designs or motion that would have to be replaced later. Hardware listening and
actual motion review remain separate from mechanical asset loading.
