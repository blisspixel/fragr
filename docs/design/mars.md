# Mars appearance and atmosphere

Production direction, 2026-09-30. Read the [bible](../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md),
[campaign](../CAMPAIGN.md) and [levels 12-14](../campaign/README.md).
These mission environments remain unbuilt.

Settlements survive around practical lifelines: habitat, contained agriculture,
industrial work and transport. The coalition's delayed cooperation should be
visible through people trying to work together, not an empty orange corridor.

| Level | Visual and emotional anchor |
|---|---|
| M12 Terms of Cooperation | Civilian habitat, greenhouse/service boundaries and meeting/work areas; urgent needs outpace coordination |
| M13 The Weight of Permission | Foundry work, material handling and controlled production; gaining the means to break the blockade |
| M14 Launch Authority | Launch services and connected settlement logistics; collective action becomes tangible departure |

Rust terrain, worn pale shielding and dark industrial frames establish the
location. Dust-filtered amber exterior light balances neutral interior work
light. Protect silhouette contrast against red ground; enemy tells cannot merge
with every wall. Pressure equipment, wind protection, hab modules and contained
plants provide local meaning. A warmer palette alone does not establish Mars.

Show repaired seals, dust accumulation by exposed thresholds, protected windows,
shared tools and cargo that later travels aboard the same ship. Factions retain
their manufacturing and markings. Cast proportions, outfits beneath added gear
and voice identities survive travel. Different movement gravity, dust hazards
and life-support failures need explicit server rules before they become play.

Water is maintained infrastructure: contained growth, recycling or industrial
use. Any spill, pool or process channel has a plausible source and authored
boundary. Open decorative blue rivers are not a shortcut to visual variety.
Use the shared water palette and pixel scale when the relevant geometry exists.
