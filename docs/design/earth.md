# Earth appearance and atmosphere

Production direction, 2026-09-30. Read the [shared bible](../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md),
[Perimeter](../lore/the-perimeter.md), [gazetteer](../lore/gazetteer.md) and
[mission index](../campaign/README.md). Existing local M01-M05 prototypes are
implementation evidence; later places and full quality remain targets.

Earth feels inhabited before it feels ruined. Humans and embodied agents have
homes, workshops, shared food, charging points and habits. The Union occupies
recognizable institutions and controls practical infrastructure. Familiar rooms
make displacement and the later return matter.

| Stage | Material/light anchor | Distinguishing detail and mood |
|---|---|---|
| M01 intake | Bone enamel, institutional green tile, dark steel, warm service lamps | Queues, counters, personal custody machinery; civic routine becomes personal danger |
| M02 correction | Issued steel, controlled enamel/green, real ceilings and local fixture pools | Restraints, processing equipment and routes between working rooms; reunion amid imposed control |
| M03 freight yard | Weathered concrete/steel and daylight | Rails, car doors, signal box, visible red mast and useful route crossovers; urgent escape through logistics |
| M04 Low Water | Worn plaster, repaired steel, cloth, muted terracotta and sage, late-afternoon sun | Homes above repair stalls, Edda's clinic, shared meal table, practical drainage and rooftop tanks; defending home while packing |
| M05 Low Water roofs | Continue M04's building forms, tanks, wear and afternoon light | Roof services, connected habitable frontage and tram trench; leaving the same community, not a new industrial district |
| M15-17 reckoning | Stadium/ceremonial scale and repeated Union manufacture | Public staging becomes combat space; visible command machinery, restrained issued red and recognizable human occupation |
| M18-20 wipe | Preserve location palette and recognizable landmarks | Sudden failures, interrupted infrastructure and unfamiliar coordinated intervention; rapid danger without instant regrowth |
| Epilogue | Same structures, opened light, weathering, clean water and muted vegetation after elapsed years | Repairs, memorials, absences and survivor use; healing without erasing the loss |

Give each room an activity, one navigable landmark and practical light. Repeat
construction families across adjacent rooms, then vary wear by use: a clinic's
clean work zone, damp service edge, repeatedly patched workshop wall. Layer wall
repairs, windows, utility runs, marks and small litter around clear combat lanes.
Do not replace meaningful room shape with a pile of clutter.

Water before the wipe reads as ordinary drains, weather and maintained storage.
Later clean water expresses recovery only alongside the explicit time change.
Cosmetic shallow puddles use existing ground; canals, flooded rooms and liquid
hazards need authored solids and server behavior. Tanks and substantial furniture
that appear to stop shots or movement require authoritative bodies.

Keep Union attack tells distinct from wall markings. Human and agent possessions
can share a space without making their bodies interchangeable. At the level 19
return, compare matched views with level 4/5 so loss is legible through changed
use and surviving landmarks, not a global color filter.
