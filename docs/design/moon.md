# Moon appearance and atmosphere

Production direction, updated 2026-10-01. The [bible](../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md) owns palette
and faction manufacture; [levels 6-9](../campaign/README.md) own encounters.
The [M06 prototype](../plans/m06-port-of-entry-prototype.md) is in flight;
later lunar missions remain unbuilt. These are linked authoring targets,
with implementation evidence and remaining acceptance recorded in that plan.

An established settlement under pressure controls, not an empty landing pad.
The port, habitation ring, custody archive and launch berth belong to the same
constructed society. People work, store belongings and maintain equipment.

| Level | Visual and emotional anchor |
|---|---|
| M06 Port of Entry | Cargo thresholds, pressure shells, dust at airlocks and controlled entry lanes; arrival meets custody infrastructure |
| M07 Declared Goods | Habitation frontage, practical service paths and crater cuts; a lived town made dangerous by curfew |
| M08 Custodian of Record | Sheltered archive, issued records equipment and personal remnants; evidence and captivity occupy the same place |
| M09 Passenger Manifest | Launch cradle, berth services and recognizable ship access; taking back transport rather than finding an unrelated spaceship |

Use pale bone/gray dust, dark basalt and weathered pressure shells. Exposed light
is hard, with deep shade; playable shadow needs sufficient fill to read bodies
and routes. Interiors have neutral and warm utility lamps. Crater rims, pressure
doors, seals, dust traps and maintenance distinguish the Moon from a gray Earth
warehouse. A bounded view of black sky is a context cue, not the entire room.

Carry faction identity through location materials: Union issued black/steel,
restrained seals and orderly lanes; civilian repair and individual possessions
around settlement life. Character bodies persist under practical environmental
gear. Gravity, vacuum, pressure damage and airlocks are server mechanic proposals
until explicitly implemented; visual framing does not secretly introduce them.

Use sparse purposeful water only in contained civilian or industrial systems.
An indoor recycling tank can explain its presence; an outdoor decorative stream
cannot stand in for an authored environmental premise. Review dust, light and
pixel density beside Earth reference assets so the game retains one style.
