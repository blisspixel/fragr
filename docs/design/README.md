# Design continuity

The linked guides translate the [art and story bible](../ART_STORY_BIBLE.md)
into consistent places, characters and scenes. They are production targets,
updated 2026-09-30, not claims that the whole campaign is built. The bible owns
the overall look, [palette](../palette.json) owns swatches,
[lore](../lore/README.md) owns fiction and [mission plans](../campaign/README.md)
own local staging. The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#full-build-order-2026-09-27) alone
owns build order.

Current material work is recorded in the [world texture expansion](../plans/world-texture-expansion.md).
Its Earth and Moon mappings support existing levels; the Mars library remains
future authoring material until a Mars mission is built and inspected.

| Guide | Applies to |
|---|---|
| [Earth](earth.md) | Levels 1-5 and 15-20, familiar Low Water and its changed return, conditional epilogue |
| [Moon](moon.md) | Levels 6-9, lunar port, town, archive and launch berth |
| [Space and ships](space.md) | Levels 10-11 and travel scenes, Common Carrier and custody tender |
| [Mars](mars.md) | Levels 12-14, habitat, foundry and launch works |
| [Characters, voices and scenes](characters.md) | Stable identity from sprites and dialogue through future cutscenes |

Each level brief has an appearance anchor pointing to its world guide. Before
authoring, expand that anchor with the room's activity, dominant materials,
light, landmark, ordinary possessions, encounter readability and continuity
change. Use the same decisions in gameplay assets, scene images, narration
and later video. A different planet changes surroundings and practical gear;
it does not silently redesign a person or faction.

The shared target is a polished 2026 retro FPS: Doom II's confident room shapes,
silhouettes and immediacy; real 3D route and movement craft; carefully authored
pixel clusters, substantial animation, practical lighting and detailed places.
Modern polish means consistency, weight, clarity and responsive presentation.
It does not require photographic materials or motion blur. Final quality is
judged in a played sequence at the actual internal resolution.
